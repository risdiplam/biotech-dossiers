03 / SCIENCE

Two platforms: antigen suppression for hepatitis, masked T-cell engagement for cancer

HDV is a defective virus that cannot make its own envelope — it coats itself in hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg). Vir attacks that dependency twice: an antibody that neutralizes HBsAg-bearing particles, and an siRNA that stops HBsAg being made. In oncology, Vir's bet is that protease-activated masking can give T-cell engagers a therapeutic window in solid tumors that the unmasked class has never had.

01

HBV co-infection

HDV infects only people with HBV. The liver produces large excesses of HBsAg, most of it in non-infectious subviral particles.

02

Envelope theft

HDV assembles its envelope from HBsAg, enters hepatocytes via the same NTCP route as HBV, and replicates using host machinery.

03

Fast progression

Chronic hepatitis delta is the most severe viral hepatitis — rapid progression to cirrhosis, liver failure and hepatocellular carcinoma; HDV was classified carcinogenic by IARC in 2025.

04

Suppression = outcome

Persistent detectable HDV RNA predicts liver events; sustained undetectability (target not detected, TND) is the field's accepted treatment goal and the endpoint hierarchy regulators have now approved drugs on.

The HDV combination in 60 seconds INTERPRETATION

Plain English. Hepatitis delta is a parasite of hepatitis B: it wraps itself in a hepatitis B protein (HBsAg) to infect liver cells. Tobevibart is an antibody that grabs that protein — blocking new infections and clearing viral particles from the blood. Elebsiran is a small interfering RNA that switches off the protein's production line inside liver cells. One drug mops up the antigen that exists; the other stops more being made. They are given as two sequential under-the-skin injections, once a month.

Technical. Tobevibart: broadly neutralizing anti-HBsAg mAb (antigenic loop epitope), Fc-engineered for effector engagement and immune-complex clearance, with Xencor Xtend half-life extension. Elebsiran (VIR-2218 / ALN-HBV02): GalNAc-conjugated siRNA licensed from Alnylam, targeting a conserved HBV transcript region shared across genotypes, degrading HBV RNAs and reducing HBsAg production, including from integrated HBV DNA to the extent transcripts share the target sequence.

PRO-XTEN masked T-cell engagers in 60 seconds INTERPRETATION

Plain English. A T-cell engager is a two-armed antibody: one arm grabs a tumor protein, the other grabs CD3 on a T cell, forcing a kill. Unmasked engagers also fire outside tumors, causing cytokine storms and damage to healthy tissue that shares the target. Vir's molecules wear protease-cut masks over both arms; the masks come off mainly where tumor-associated proteases are active — inside the tumor — so the drug should be inert in the blood and active at the cancer.

Technical. PRO-XTEN (originated at Amunix, acquired by Sanofi in 2021, licensed exclusively to Vir for oncology and infectious disease in 2024): peptide masks tethered by protease-cleavable linkers block both the tumor-antigen and CD3 binding sites; tumor-microenvironment proteases cleave the linkers and unmask the bispecific. Masking also prolongs circulating half-life of the inactive prodrug. Vir layers its dAIsY AI/protein-engineering platform on candidate design — a company capability claim, not independently validated. UNVERIFIED

1 · Antigen sources the siRNA can't reach

HBsAg is also expressed from HBV DNA integrated into the host genome. If integrated transcripts escape elebsiran's target site in a patient subset, antigen production persists, HDV keeps enveloping, and TND plateaus — one plausible read of why suppression deepens slowly over 96 weeks rather than saturating early.

2 · Virology ≠ the composite endpoint

Approvals in HDV now turn on virologic response plus ALT normalization. ALT reflects liver inflammation with many drivers (including underlying HBV activity and steatosis); a patient can be TND and still fail the composite. SOLSTICE's Week-24 composite favored monotherapy — the ALT leg, not the viral leg, is the combination's weak point.

3 · Masking cuts both ways

The same mask that prevents systemic activation can blunt potency if tumor protease activity is heterogeneous, and can leak if proteases are active in inflamed healthy tissue. CRS still occurred in 50% of VIR-5500 patients (mostly grade 1) — masking reduces, and has not eliminated, class toxicity. No masked TCE has ever been approved, anywhere.

4 · Target biology in healthy tissue

PSMA is expressed in salivary glands, kidney and gut; HER2 in cardiac and pulmonary tissue (the trastuzumab lesson); EGFR in skin and GI mucosa. Dual masking must protect all of these simultaneously, at doses high enough to work in poorly perfused solid tumors — the central unproven claim of the whole oncology pivot.

HDV combination safety

FACT In SOLSTICE, the combination arm had no grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events and no treatment-related discontinuations through Week 96; treatment-emergent events were generally mild-to-moderate and transient. This is a genuinely clean profile for a chronic monthly regimen — if it holds in the larger ECLIPSE population.

VIR-5500 safety

FACT Phase 1 monotherapy (n=58): no dose-limiting toxicities; grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 12% (7/58); CRS 50% (29/58), predominantly grade 1 fever, without routine prophylactic steroids. Better than historical unmasked TCE experience, not benign.

What remains unknowable

GAP No masked-TCE durability data (response duration for VIR-5500 has not been reported), no randomized data for any Vir program except the controlled ECLIPSE trials now reading out, and no human data at all for VIR-5525 efficacy or the Gilead discovery candidates.

04 / CLINICAL

SOLSTICE: the deepest HDV suppression on record — with an endpoint paradox inside it

The Phase 2 SOLSTICE trial randomized 65 adults with chronic hepatitis delta to the combination (tobevibart 300 mg + elebsiran 200 mg, every 4 weeks; n=32) or tobevibart monotherapy (300 mg every 2 weeks; n=33), open label. It was published in the New England Journal of Medicine in 2026 — and its details matter more than its headline.

The honest tension

Why take the combination into Phase 3 at all?

Because TND — complete suppression — is what predicts liver outcomes, and only the combination gets most patients there and keeps them there (mono TND is flat at ~53% from Week 72 to 96; the combination climbs). The Week-24 composite punished the combination on ALT normalization timing, not on virology. INTERPRETATION That is a coherent scientific case. It is also a post-hoc reordering of endpoint importance relative to the trial's own primary — exactly the kind of argument regulators discount, and ECLIPSE 1's Week-48 timing gives ALT two more quarters to normalize. Whether that was foresight or luck is what Q4 2026 answers.

MARCH (hepatitis B): real HBsAg loss, sub-commercial functional cure FACT

The Phase 2 MARCH study (NCT04856085) tested the same doublet ± weekly pegylated interferon-α in chronic hepatitis B. At 24 weeks post-treatment (EASL 2025): HBsAg loss in the low-baseline-HBsAg (<1,000 IU/mL) subgroup was 17% (3/18) doublet and 21% (3/14) triplet — but study-defined functional cure was 4% (2/51) and 10% (3/30) across all participants. Vir has stated Phase 3 in HBV proceeds only with a global partner; none has been secured in the ~17 months since the data. The program is carried in this dossier as an option, not a value driver.

05 / ECLIPSE

The registrational program: fully enrolled, three controlled trials

13 / RED FLAGS

Ranked: what can break the thesis, in order

Ranking weighs probability against value at risk, as of Oct 16, 2026. INTERPRETATION

01

The composite endpoint already failed the combination once

SOLSTICE's prespecified Week-24 primary — virologic response plus ALT normalization — read 70% monotherapy vs 47% combination. There was no prespecified statistical testing, and the combination's case rests on later TND separation. ECLIPSE 1 moves the composite to Week 48 against deferred treatment, but the ALT leg remains the regimen's demonstrated weak point in the only randomized dataset that exists. FACT (data) / INTERPRETATION (weight)

02

Third to market in the lead indication

Hepcludex is approved in the US (2026); brelovitug has positive Phase 3 composite data (p<.0001) and a guided H1 2027 filing as a single-agent monthly antibody. Vir's two-drug monthly combo must differentiate on depth of suppression alone, against entrenched and simpler alternatives, while self-funding US commercialization. FACT

03

Economics are shared at every layer

Alnylam royalties and up to $395M of milestones sit on elebsiran; Sanofi takes 20% of certain Astellas proceeds and holds TCE milestones/royalties; Norgine owns HDV commercialization across Europe/Australia/NZ; Astellas takes half of US VIR-5500 profit and all of ex-US. Reported "up to" deal values overstate what reaches Vir shareholders by several turns. FACT

04

Dilution is the business model's quiet half

Shares rose ~21% in seven months of 2026 (offering at a ~$8.50 implied net price; partner equity at $10.36–$14.16). An ATM is on file. A US HDV launch, an ECLIPSE filing, and a VIR-5500 Phase 3 are all ahead of the cash curve's end (H2 2028) — expect the share count, not just the science, to compound. FACT / INTERPRETATION

05

Masked TCEs remain an unproven class

No protease-masked TCE has been approved; VIR-5500's durability is unreported; CRS still occurs in half of patients (mostly grade 1); VIR-5818 and VIR-5525 have no efficacy data. The platform's market value currently capitalizes a Phase 1 PSA-response signal. FACT

06

HBV is a stranded asset absent a partner

MARCH functional cure of 4–10% overall has not attracted a Phase 3 partner in ~17 months. Either the data are below the partnering bar or Vir's terms are — both readings cap the program's value near zero until proven otherwise. INTERPRETATION

07

C-suite churn into the binary

CFO and CMO both changed in October 2026, weeks before ECLIPSE 1 topline. Individually explicable (growth-stage finance and clinical leadership for a pivotal year); jointly, they remove the two officers most associated with the readout's delivery and interpretation. FACT / INTERPRETATION

What this dossier could not close

Stated plainly, in the order they matter.

Option strike schedule

GAP The Q2 2026 10-Q anti-dilutive table gives option and restricted-share counts but the weighted-average exercise prices were not extracted, so a true treasury-method fully diluted count cannot be computed. Scenarios use the gross 186.6M; the treasury-method figure would be lower and per-share scenario values correspondingly higher.

Norgine economics

GAP The December 2025 Norgine license (HDV commercialization, Europe/Australia/NZ, plus ECLIPSE cost-sharing) has no disclosed upfront, milestone or royalty terms in the sources reviewed; the EU value split of the lead asset is therefore unpriced in this dossier.

VIR-5500 durability

GAP Duration of response, rPFS and ≥3000 µg/kg cohort follow-up beyond the ASCO GU 2026 cut are not public. The base and bull cases both lean on durability that has not yet been shown.

ECLIPSE 1 analysis details

GAP The statistical analysis plan for the Week-48 composite (handling of the deferred arm's pre-switch window, missing-data rules, cirrhosis subgroup power) has not been published; SOLSTICE's descriptive-only precedent argues for caution until the topline release's methods are visible.

Gilead deal closing

GAP Closing conditions and expected closing date for the October 2026 Gilead agreement ($65M cash, $25M equity at $14.16) are not stated in the 8-K summary reviewed; pro-forma figures here assume it closes as signed.

SOLSTICE registration identifier

GAP SOLSTICE's ClinicalTrials.gov identifier was not confirmed in the sources pulled for this dossier (MARCH is NCT04856085; the ECLIPSE and VIR-5500 identifiers are stated in their sections).

Bottom line

INTERPRETATION Vir is two asymmetric bets sharing one balance sheet. The HDV bet is near, binary, and no longer first: the science (deep, durable TND suppression) is the best in the field on the measure that predicts outcomes, but the value will be decided by a composite endpoint on which the combination has already trailed once, against competitors who have moved first. The TCE bet is earlier, partnered twice over, and priced on a Phase 1 signal whose durability is unshown. At ~$764M of enterprise value with ~$5.96/share of cash beneath it, the market is paying for the platform and asking the HDV readout to prove it deserves to. Q4 2026 answers the first half of that sentence.
