The bull case in one paragraph

  • There is no approved targeted therapy for EGFR C797S after osimertinib; C797S occurs in ~7% of first-line and up to ~26% of later-line osimertinib resistance in the literature.
  • BH-30643 was purpose-built for exactly this: non-covalent (so it does not need the C797 cysteine osimertinib loses), macrocyclic (the design language of founder Jean Cui's lorlatinib and repotrectinib), mutant-selective and brain-active by design.
  • Early data point the right way: 45% ORR (95% CI 29–62%), 88% disease control, 63% still on treatment at 6.9 months median follow-up, mostly Grade 1 wild-type EGFR toxicity.
  • The founder has designed three FDA-approved drugs. That is a real, checkable track record, rare at a ~$443M market cap.

What the story does not remove

  • n=40, single-arm, short follow-up: the CI runs from 29% to 62%, and durability (DoR/PFS) — what accelerated approval actually turns on — is not yet reported.
  • C797S is a shrinking, contested niche: front-line osimertinib combinations are already reducing C797S emergence, and post-osimertinib standards (amivantamab + chemotherapy) do not require the mutation.
  • The stock priced at $16, traded to $27.99, and sits below IPO price — a broken IPO tape with a ~180-day lock-up expiry ahead in early 2027.
  • Two of three programs are pre-Phase 2; the KRAS drug has no IND yet. Runway into Q2 2028 does not fund an approval.

02 / SCIENCE

Why a macrocycle, why non-covalent — and how it fails

Plain version first, then the technical version.

Plain English. INTERPRETATION EGFR is a growth switch stuck "on" in some lung cancers. Osimertinib, the standard drug, works by permanently gluing itself to one spot on the switch (a cysteine called C797). Cancer escapes by changing that single spot to serine (C797S) — the glue no longer sticks. BH-30643 does not use glue at all: it is a ring-shaped molecule contoured to fit the mutated switch's active shape and hold it off reversibly, while largely ignoring the normal (wild-type) switch in skin and gut — which is where the rash and diarrhea of older EGFR drugs come from.
Technical — BH-30643. FACT BH-30643 is an orally bioavailable, macrocyclic, non-covalent, mutant-selective "OMNI-EGFR" inhibitor designed for super-potency across classical activating mutations (exon 19 deletion, L858R), atypical/PACC mutations, exon 20 insertions, and resistance genotypes including C797S with or without T790M, while sparing wild-type EGFR and HER2 by selectively targeting the active kinase conformation. Because binding is non-covalent, it is mechanistically independent of the C797 thiol that third-generation covalent TKIs require. Company disclosures report high plasma exposures exceeding target EC90 at candidate doses and describe the drug as brain-active. UNVERIFIED Potency/selectivity multiples are company-reported from preclinical work, not independently replicated in the materials reviewed.
Why it can fail. INTERPRETATION (1) Non-covalent binding trades the lost covalent anchor for an affinity problem — sustained target coverage depends on exposure, and the dose cohorts tested spanned 20–160 mg total daily dose, with expansion at 40–60 mg twice daily. (2) On-target escape does not stop at C797S: the fourth-generation literature already documents second-site mutations (e.g., L718X, Q791L) conferring cross-resistance to this class preclinically. (3) Most post-osimertinib resistance is not C797S at all — MET amplification, bypass signaling, histologic transformation and unknown mechanisms dominate FLAURA-era data — so a C797S drug addresses a minority slice of progressing patients. (4) The most common treatment-related adverse event reported was bilirubin elevation attributed to UGT1A1 inhibition (a Gilbert's-like effect): mostly asymptomatic in Phase 1, but a real drug-interaction and labeling consideration.
Technical — BH-30236 (CLK). FACT BH-30236 is an oral macrocyclic inhibitor of CDC-like kinases (CLKs), which phosphorylate splicing factors and thereby regulate alternative mRNA splicing. Aberrant splicing is a hallmark of myeloid malignancies, especially spliceosome-mutant AML/MDS, and splicing modulation is a documented mechanism of resistance to venetoclax, FLT3 inhibitors and chemotherapy. At clinically relevant concentrations BH-30236 also inhibits DYRK1A/1B/2, PIM3 and FLT3 in preclinical studies (company data). The failure mode is class-typical: splicing is essential in normal cells too, so the therapeutic window — not target engagement — is the open question, and no CLK inhibitor has ever been approved.
Technical — BH-501284 (pan-KRAS). FACT BH-501284 is an oral, non-covalent, selective pan-KRAS Switch-II pocket inhibitor with a novel scaffold and "pseudo-irreversible" binding intended to produce prolonged target engagement. Approved KRAS drugs (sotorasib, adagrasib) are covalent and G12C-only. A non-covalent pan-KRAS agent would cover G12D/V/R and other alleles — and would also inhibit wild-type KRAS in normal tissue, which is the central tolerability gamble of every pan-KRAS program. Company-reported preclinical data (AACR, Apr 2026; AACR Pancreatic Cancer, Sep 2026) describe sustained engagement and tumor regression at low doses in KRAS-mutant models. UNVERIFIED Preclinical only; no human data; IND guided Q1 2027.

04 / TRIALS

What has actually been tested in humans

Two trials. Everything else is animal data.

SOLARA — BH-30643, Phase 1/2, EGFR-mutant NSCLC. FACT Open-label Phase 1/2 in locally advanced/metastatic NSCLC with EGFR (and/or HER2) mutations. Phase 1 dose escalation/backfill enrolled patients across 20–160 mg total daily dose; the ASCO 2026 cut (Mar 2, 2026 data cut, efficacy follow-up to Apr 29) covered 82 escalation/backfill patients (median 3 prior lines, 66% history of brain metastases, 71% ECOG 1). Phase 2 is designed to test antitumor efficacy and safety in mutation/treatment-history cohorts at the recommended Phase 2 dose. The trial's ClinicalTrials.gov identifier was not in the materials reviewed. GAP
SOLARA — C797S efficacy (IASLC/WCLC 2026 update). FACT As of the May 12, 2026 data cut (efficacy follow-up through Aug 10, 2026): 40 patients with C797S resistance to prior EGFR inhibitors, with or without concurrent T790M (35% had concurrent T790M); median 2 prior lines; 98% prior osimertinib; 53% prior chemotherapy and/or ADC; 53% history of brain metastases. 18 patients achieved confirmed (16) or ongoing unconfirmed (2) partial responses — ORR 45% (95% CI 29–62%); disease control rate 88% (35/40); 25 patients (63%) remained on treatment; median follow-up 6.9 months. Duration of response and PFS were not reported in this cut. GAP No DoR/PFS yet — the single most important missing number for an accelerated-approval argument.
SOLARA — safety at expansion doses. FACT Among 174 patients treated at 40, 50 or 60 mg twice daily: treatment-related dose reductions 9%, discontinuations 3%; wild-type EGFR-associated treatment-related events were primarily Grade 1; the most common treatment-related event was bilirubin elevation, generally asymptomatic and predominantly unconjugated, consistent with UGT1A1 inhibition. The earlier ASCO cut reported Grade ≥2 wild-type EGFR treatment-related events in 27% and no clinically significant cardiac effects.
BH-30236-01 — Phase 1/1b, R/R AML / HR-MDS (NCT06501196). FACT First-in-human, multicenter, open-label dose escalation and expansion, planned enrollment ~170: Part 1 monotherapy escalation (~50 patients), Part 2 escalation in combination with venetoclax (~48), Phase 1b expansion up to 72, as monotherapy or with venetoclax. Adults with R/R AML or higher-risk MDS, 1–5 prior lines, ECOG ≤2; prior CLK inhibitor excluded. Recruiting.
BH-30236 — EHA 2026 initial data. UNVERIFIED Company-reported (GlobeNewswire, Jun 11, 2026): generally well tolerated, predictable PK without accumulation and no significant venetoclax drug-interaction signal; early anti-leukemic activity including after prior venetoclax. Secondary summaries of the May 2026 update reported ≥50% bone-marrow blast reductions in 29% of evaluable monotherapy patients and 55% of evaluable combination patients, including one MRD-negative complete remission in a patient refractory to prior venetoclax — on single-digit evaluable denominators (n=5 reported in each summary line). Treat the percentages as directional only; the denominators are too small to carry a thesis.
05 / REGULATORY

Designations are not approvals

BlossomHill has collected the two designations available at its stage. Neither shortens the evidence bar by itself.

  • FACT Fast Track — BH-30643 for advanced/metastatic EGFR C797S-positive NSCLC after a third-generation EGFR TKI (granted 2026, announced with Q2 results).
  • FACT Orphan Drug — BH-30236 for AML (granted March 2026).
  • FACT End-of-Phase 1 meeting guided Q4 2026 — agenda per the company: recommended Phase 2 dose selection and a potential accelerated-approval pathway in C797S resistance. First patient in the anticipated pivotal Phase 2 guided Q1 2027.
  • INTERPRETATION Accelerated approval on single-arm ORR requires responses that are large, durable, and in a population with no available therapy. A 45% ORR at 6.9 months median follow-up, without reported DoR, is the opening argument — not the conclusion. If FDA requires a randomized confirmatory-first design, timelines and capital needs change materially.
  • GAP No Breakthrough Therapy designation has been disclosed. No FDA interaction minutes are public. Whether a single-arm trial can support accelerated approval here is genuinely unknown until the EoP1 outcome is disclosed.

Bear — $4–6

Bull — $30–40. 11 / RISKS

Ranked red flags

Ordered by capacity to destroy the thesis, not by probability.

1

Durability is unreported — and accelerated approval is a durability argument

ORR 45% at 6.9 months median follow-up, DoR/PFS not yet disclosed. If responses are short, the EoP1 meeting and the whole C797S thesis deflate together. FACT + INTERPRETATION

2

The addressable niche is a subset of a subset, and it is shrinking

C797S is ~7% of first-line osimertinib progressors in FLAURA-era data; front-line combinations reduce it further; the mutation-agnostic post-osimertinib standard does not need BLSM's biomarker. Trial success does not fix market geometry. FACT

3

Single-arm, n=40, wide confidence interval

45% ORR (95% CI 29–62%) in a heterogeneous, heavily pretreated population (53% prior chemo/ADC, 53% brain metastases) with 2 of 18 responses unconfirmed at the cut. Regression toward the CI's lower half on expansion is the statistical norm, not the exception. FACT

4

Class resistance biology is already documented

Peer-reviewed profiling shows fourth-generation EGFR inhibitors face second-site cross-resistance (L718X, Q791L). BH-30643 has no human data yet on post-treatment resistance. FACT

5

Broken IPO tape + lock-up overhang

Priced $16, high $27.99, now $13.94 — public holders are underwater, the ~Feb 2027 lock-up expiry releases low-basis pre-IPO shares, and short interest more than tripled in the second half of September (to ~1.5% — small, but the direction is informed money hedging the EoP1). FACT / UNVERIFIED (short interest)

6

CLK has no approved precedent and a window problem

Splicing is essential biology; BH-30236's human data are single-digit-denominator blast-count summaries. A narrow window or a serious safety signal ends the second leg quietly. INTERPRETATION

7

Pan-KRAS is a race BLSM starts years behind

Preclinical, IND not yet filed, against a Phase 3 pan-RAS competitor — and pan-KRAS means wild-type KRAS inhibition in normal tissue by design. FACT

8

Runway ends before approval does

Into Q2 2028 funds a pivotal start, not a finish. At least one dilutive raise is the base case; the FD share count is undisclosed in this pass precisely because the option pool that will dilute first is real. INTERPRETATION

9

Key-person concentration

The design thesis of the entire company is substantially one chemist's macrocycle platform. Cui's record is the bull case; her irreplaceability is the corresponding risk. 63 employees at listing concentrates execution risk further. INTERPRETATION

10

UGT1A1 / bilirubin and DDI management

The most common treatment-related event is a UGT1A1-inhibition bilirubin elevation. Benign in Phase 1 summaries; in a registrational program it invites drug-interaction studies, monitoring language, and exclusion-criteria friction. FACT

12 / OPEN

The evidence that would change the view

What to watch, in order.

  • EoP1 outcome language (Q4 2026): "FDA agreed a single-arm trial may support accelerated approval" versus any randomized-trial language is the whole ballgame.
  • DoR/PFS at the 1H 2027 SOLARA update: median DoR beyond ~9–12 months converts ORR into an approval argument; early censoring-heavy curves do not.
  • CNS-specific response data: 53% of the C797S cohort had brain-metastasis history; intracranial ORR is a genuine differentiator if disclosed.
  • Net IPO proceeds and Q3 cash (next 10-Q): closes the GAP in this dossier's EV math.
  • BH-30236 expansion data with real denominators: CR/CRh rates, not blast-count percentages on n=5.
  • BH-501284 IND acceptance (Q1 2027) and first-in-human dose-escalation tolerability — the wild-type KRAS window question.
  • Open-market insider buying below $16 — its absence so far is noted above.
