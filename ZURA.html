01 / FRAME

The question is whether two weak pathways add up to one strong drug

Neither parent antibody proved decisive alone: tabalumab (anti-BAFF) failed Phase 3 in rheumatoid arthritis and in lupus, and IL-17A blockade alone gives mid-tier hidradenitis responses. Zura's bet is that simultaneous, near-complete suppression of both — demonstrated pharmacodynamically, not yet clinically — breaks an efficacy ceiling. TibuSHIELD is the first real test of that bet in any disease.

The bull case in one chain

  • BAFF is not a decorative target in HS: lesional skin shows neutrophil-derived BAFF supporting B/plasma-cell infiltrates, at levels above psoriasis or atopic dermatitis in published work.
  • BAFF is also elevated in systemic sclerosis, correlates with skin fibrosis extent, and BAFF inhibition attenuated fibrosis in a scleroderma mouse model.
  • Phase 1/1b: >98% median trough target engagement for both targets at 300 mg every 4 weeks, half-life 26.9 days, low ADA — the molecule does engage both arms at a monthly dose.
  • Both Phase 2 trials over-enrolled (247 vs ~225 planned in HS; 91 vs ~80 in SSc), and cash covers both readouts plus a third indication without a forced raise.

02 / SCIENCE

IL-17 drives the flare; BAFF keeps the B-cell factory open

HS and diffuse cutaneous SSc are both diseases where innate inflammation and adaptive autoimmunity reinforce each other. Tibulizumab is engineered to cut both loops with one molecule — a rational design whose clinical payoff is still a hypothesis.

Tibulizumab in 60 seconds

Plain English. Two signals keep these diseases going. IL-17A tells skin and tissue to recruit neutrophils and inflame. BAFF tells B cells — the cells that make antibodies and help sustain chronic lesions — to survive instead of dying off. Tibulizumab is one antibody built to soak up both signals at once, given as a monthly under-the-skin injection. The hope is that shutting both doors works better than shutting either one alone.

Technical. FACT A tetravalent IgG-scFv bispecific (LY3090106 / ZB-106): the anti-BAFF antibody tabalumab as the IgG backbone, with an anti-IL-17A single-chain variable fragment derived from ixekizumab fused via a glycine-rich linker. Benschop et al. (mAbs 2019) describe simultaneous binding to both cytokines, engineering to fix scFv aggregation and chemical degradation (CDR residue replacement, charge balancing, an H44–L100 disulfide), potent antagonism in cell and mouse models, B-cell suppression in cynomolgus monkey, and intact prolonged circulation. It neutralises IL-17A and IL-17A/F heterodimers; it does not directly neutralise IL-17F homodimers — a mechanistic gap versus bimekizumab and sonelokimab, which block A and F.

01

IL-17A in HS

Th17/IL-17 signalling is a validated HS axis: secukinumab and bimekizumab are approved. Validation cuts both ways — the axis works, and the bar is already set by marketed drugs.

02

BAFF in HS lesions

FACT Sabat et al., J Allergy Clin Immunol 2023: neutrophil-derived BAFF is highly expressed in HS nodules, abscesses and fistulas, supports lesional B/plasma cells via BAFF-R, TACI and BCMA, and BAFF neutralisation reversed B-cell activation ex vivo.

03

BAFF in SSc

FACT Matsushita et al., Arthritis Rheum 2006 (PMID 16385515): serum BAFF elevated in 83 SSc patients, correlated with skin fibrosis; falling BAFF tracked skin regression in longitudinal samples. François et al., Sci Adv 2018: BAFF inhibition shifted effector/regulatory B-cell balance and attenuated fibrosis in mice.

04

Why PMR is next

UNVERIFIED · COMPANY Zura cites IL-17 and BAFF evidence in polymyalgia rheumatica. The proven steroid-sparing axis in PMR is IL-6R (sarilumab, SAPHYR), not IL-17 or BAFF — PMR is the least clinically validated of the three indications.

1 · The BAFF parent already failed

Tabalumab was discontinued in RA (2013, interim futility) and in SLE (2014, ILLUMINATE Phase 3 efficacy shortfall). BAFF suppression alone has repeatedly produced biomarker movement without clinically meaningful efficacy. Tibulizumab must show the combination, not the target, is the fix.

2 · No IL-17F homodimer coverage

Bimekizumab (IL-17A+F) and sonelokimab (A+F) set the modern HS bar. If IL-17F contributes materially in HS skin, tibulizumab's A-only neutralisation starts at a mechanistic deficit that BAFF must more than repay.

3 · Fibrosis may not reverse in 24 weeks

In early dcSSc, mRSS improves spontaneously in a substantial minority, and established fibrosis is slow to remodel. A 24-week mRSS primary in ~91 patients can miss a real anti-fibrotic effect — or manufacture a false one from natural history and regression to the mean.

4 · Dual immunosuppression, dual infection surface

IL-17 blockade carries candidiasis and IBD-caution baggage; BAFF suppression lowers immunoglobulins and B cells over time. Seventy-eight Phase 1 participants cannot exclude the combined long-term infection profile of chronic dual suppression.

Key literature: Benschop et al., mAbs 2019 (PMC6748573) · Sabat et al., JACI 2023 · Matsushita et al., Arthritis Rheum 2006 · François et al., Sci Adv 2018 (PMC6040844). Tabalumab history: Lilly discontinuation reports, 2013–2014.

03 / CLINICAL

Two enrolled Phase 2 trials, one planned — and no efficacy readout yet

Everything clinical about Zura today is design, pharmacodynamics and safety. The first efficacy number the company will ever report for tibulizumab is the TibuSHIELD Week 16 result.

Phase 1/1b: what is actually known

UNVERIFIED · COMPANY / LILLY CSRs 78 participants dosed across three Phase 1/1b studies (RA, Sjögren's). At 300 mg Q4W: >98% median trough target engagement for both IL-17 and BAFF, half-life 26.9 days, B-cell and CRP reductions, low treatment-emergent ADA, no new safety signals versus known IL-17/BAFF inhibitor experience. These are company-reported summaries of Lilly-conducted studies, not a peer-reviewed efficacy dataset.

Regulatory path

FACT No approval, no disclosed breakthrough or fast-track designation for tibulizumab in any indication as of this dossier. 10 / RISKS

Ranked red flags

Ordered by capacity to destroy the thesis, not by probability.

  1. Zero human efficacy data, anywhereThe entire $329M economic EV rests on pharmacodynamics (target engagement, B-cell counts) from 78 Phase 1 participants. Engagement is not efficacy — tabalumab engaged BAFF for a decade without a Phase 3 win.
  2. The parent-antibody graveyardTabalumab failed Phase 3 in RA and SLE; ixekizumab's HS-relevant axis is real but mid-tier as monotherapy. The bispecific must be more than the sum of two disappointing-to-average parts.
  3. HS competitive velocitySonelokimab Phase 3 HiSCR75 ~67% at Week 52 with a BLA expected, povorcitinib Phase 3-positive, tulisokibart Phase 2b HiSCR50 72% vs 35%. Tibulizumab can succeed and still arrive third-in-class into a repriced market.
  4. Primary-endpoint optics riskTibuSHIELD's primary (percent change in AN count) is not the field's headline metric. A statistical win with ordinary HiSCR50/75 secondaries is the most dangerous “positive” outcome — it funds a Phase 3 the market may not reward.
  5. No IL-17F homodimer blockadeBoth modern IL-17 competitors block A+F. If F matters in HS skin, tibulizumab is mechanistically outgunned before BAFF enters the argument.
  6. SSc trial fragility~91 patients, 24 weeks, mRSS primary — small, short, and exposed to spontaneous improvement in early dcSSc. A miss here is weak evidence against the drug; a hit is weak evidence for it. Price will treat both as stronger than they are.
  7. Pre-funded warrant and option overhang29.3M pre-funded warrants ($0.001) plus 21.2M options on a 95.4M issued base. Headline market caps using issued shares understate economic equity value by ~$125M at $4.28.
  8. Serial dilution is the business model so farIssued shares: 73.7M (Dec 2025) → 95.4M (Jun 2026) in one raise; $114M ATM still available; any Phase 3 requires a raise well beyond the guided 2028 runway.
  9. Leadership turnoverThree CEOs and two CFOs since 2024, including a CEO medical leave mid-execution, and the founding CEO's exit from the board in May 2026. The Feb 2026 board additions mitigate, but continuity risk into two binary readouts is real.

What this dossier could not resolve

Disclosed gaps, in the open, rather than smoothed over.

GAP 01

Registry dose labels and the SSc count

NCT06993610 names its arms only “Dose A” and “Dose B” — the 150 mg / 300 mg mapping comes from the company's Aug 2026 presentation, not the registry. The HS registry was updated Oct 19, 2026 to actuals (247 enrolled; primary completion Sep 18, 2026, actual). The SSc registry (NCT06843239) still shows the original 80-patient estimate against the company's 91 randomised — the 91 figure remains company-reported.

GAP 02

Legacy settlement economics

The Dec 29, 2025 settlements (Stone Peach / BAFFX17 / Athanor) terminated the legacy founder-era agreements; only the BAFFX17 leg is quantified in the materials reviewed here (a $5.0M accrued obligation reversed, per the FY2025 results release). The full consideration and any contingent payments under the companion settlements sit in the Jan 2026 8-K and were not re-verified line-by-line for this dossier.

GAP 03

NEXUS-PMR registration

No NCT number, sample size, or dose design for the PMR trial was identified as of Oct 16, 2026 — only the company-stated Week 52 sustained-remission endpoint and YE 2026 start target.

Verdict

A clean, funded coin-flip on a genuinely differentiated mechanism — priced like a favourite.

Zura has done the things a pre-data company can do: enrolled two trials past target, raised money before it needed it, and kept runway past both readouts. What it has not done — what no one has done — is show that blocking IL-17A and BAFF together helps a single patient more than blocking IL-17 alone. At ~$329M of economic enterprise value with sonelokimab, povorcitinib and tulisokibart all posting numbers first, TibuSHIELD does not need to be positive; it needs to be deep. A mid-tier HiSCR print is the bear case wearing a positive headline.
