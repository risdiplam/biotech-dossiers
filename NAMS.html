01 / FRAME

The question is whether LDL-C lowering by this mechanism converts to events — again

Four CETP inhibitors reached Phase 3 before obicetrapib. One increased mortality, two were futile, and the one that worked (anacetrapib, REVEAL) was abandoned for fat accumulation. Obicetrapib is the most ApoB-potent molecule the class has produced and the first to be approved anywhere — on a lipid endpoint. PREVAIL is the trial that decides whether it is a drug or another footnote.

Why this molecule is not torcetrapib

  • Potency where it matters: LDL-C −32.6% (BROADWAY) to −41.5% at one year (BROOKLYN), ApoB −19% to −26%, Lp(a) −33% to −54% — the earlier failures either barely lowered ApoB (dalcetrapib) or carried off-target toxicity (torcetrapib's aldosterone/blood-pressure effect, absent here).
  • REVEAL proved the mechanism can work: anacetrapib reduced major coronary events (RR 0.91, p=0.004) in 30,449 patients, proportional to ApoB lowering — genetics (Ference et al.) says CETP benefit tracks ApoB, not HDL.
  • Regulators have now seen the full package: the EC approved on three Phase 3 lipid trials published in NEJM, The Lancet, and Nature Medicine.
  • The balance sheet does not force a bad decision: $678.3M of cash against ~$35M/quarter of H1 2026 operating cash use funds PREVAIL to completion without a raise at current burn.

What the EU approval does not remove

  • Approval was granted on LDL-C, a surrogate. No CETP inhibitor has ever shown an outcomes benefit in its own sponsor's pivotal CVOT except anacetrapib — modestly, in a trial Merck chose not to file.
  • The PREVAIL interim is a genuine binary: futility or a weak trend at interim reprices the U.S. opportunity toward zero, because there is no disclosed U.S. filing path that does not run through outcomes data.
  • Europe is a royalty stream (low-double-digits to mid-20s percent of Menarini's net sales), not product revenue — the largest market, the U.S., is retained but unbuilt and unfiled.
  • Merck's oral PCSK9 (enlicitide, LIPFENDRA) is FDA-approved (Jul 2026) and already occupies the "potent oral" slot in the U.S. — without a class graveyard behind it.
  • The stock fell ~43% in 2026 into its own approval — the market is paying for the interim risk, not ignoring it.

03 / SCIENCE

CETP: the target that ate four Phase 3 programs

CETP shuttles cholesteryl esters from HDL to ApoB-containing particles (VLDL/LDL) in exchange for triglycerides. Blocking it raises HDL-C dramatically and — only if the molecule is potent enough — lowers LDL-C and ApoB. The class's history is a controlled experiment in which of those effects matters: events track ApoB, not HDL.

01

CETP transfer

CETP moves cholesteryl esters from HDL into ApoB lipoproteins. High CETP activity = cholesterol loaded onto the particles that drive atherosclerosis.

02

Blockade

Obicetrapib (10 mg, once daily, oral) is a highly selective CETP inhibitor. HDL-C rises ~+139% (BROOKLYN) because cholesteryl esters stay in HDL.

03

ApoB clearance

A stable-isotope tracer study (Bajaj, Rader et al.) found obicetrapib substantially increases LDL-ApoB fractional clearance — the liver removes ApoB particles faster; production is not the driver.

04

Net lipid effect

LDL-C −33% to −42%, ApoB −19% to −26%, non-HDL-C ~−33%, Lp(a) −33% to −54% across Phase 3. Whether that converts to fewer MACE is the PREVAIL question.

CETP inhibition in 60 seconds INTERPRETATION

Plain English. Think of CETP as a courier moving cholesterol from the "return" trucks (HDL) onto the "delivery" trucks (LDL and relatives) that deposit it in artery walls. Obicetrapib stops the courier. Cholesterol stays on the return trucks (HDL soars — a cosmetic effect), and, more importantly, the liver clears the delivery trucks faster, so fewer ApoB particles circulate. Fewer ApoB particles over years should mean fewer heart attacks. "Should" is doing real work in that sentence — it is exactly what PREVAIL is testing.

Technical. Obicetrapib is a potent, selective, orally bioavailable CETP inhibitor dosed at 10 mg QD. Unlike torcetrapib it shows no aldosterone-mediated blood-pressure signal (TANDEM pre-specified systolic BP analyses were neutral); unlike anacetrapib it is not known to accumulate in adipose tissue — though, as with any long-half-life lipophilic molecule, multi-decade tissue exposure is unobservable in 1-year trials and remains a monitored unknown rather than a demonstrated negative. Mendelian randomization (Ference et al., JAMA 2017) shows genetically lower CETP activity reduces cardiovascular risk in proportion to ApoB/LDL lowering, the same proportionality as statins and PCSK9 — the strongest available evidence that the mechanism, dosed hard enough, should work.

1 · Magnitude × duration

Event reduction scales with absolute ApoB reduction × years of exposure (Cholesterol Treatment Trialists' proportionality). BROADWAY's exploratory 21% MACE reduction at one year (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.54–1.15) is far larger than proportionality predicts — expect regression toward ~10% in PREVAIL's larger, longer dataset; the trial's blinded event rate has run lower than expected, which is why the company extended event accrual and added the Q4 2026 interim (Q2 2026 10-Q).

2 · The interim boundary

Early stopping requires overwhelming benefit on both the 4-point primary and 3-point MACE. A "positive trend, continue" outcome — statistically the most likely good outcome — gives the market no U.S. filing catalyst until the final readout.

3 · Kidney signal to watch

FACT In BROADWAY, mean eGFR declined significantly more on obicetrapib than placebo; in the pooled BROADWAY/BROOKLYN analysis, categorical eGFR-decline events were actually less frequent on drug — a mixed signal — even as new-onset diabetes and glycemic worsening were less frequent on obicetrapib. Small renal effects in a chronic, elderly, comorbid population are a label-negotiation topic if PREVAIL is positive.

4 · HDL is decoration

If any part of the investment case rests on the +139% HDL-C, discard it: dalcetrapib and evacetrapib both raised HDL massively and did nothing. The pooled mediation analysis (company; BROADWAY+BROOKLYN) attributes 84.5% of the observed MACE effect jointly to LDL-C and Lp(a) — that is the only mechanistic bridge worth underwriting, and it is post hoc.

04 / CLINICAL

Three clean lipid trials, one unfinished outcomes trial

BROADWAY, BROOKLYN, and TANDEM all hit their primary endpoints with p<0.0001 and placebo-like tolerability, and all three are published in top journals. None was powered for events. PREVAIL is the only outcomes evidence that will ever exist for this molecule — its design and interim mechanics deserve more attention than the lipid data.

Real biomarker signal, unproven indication

The EU pathway is complete and first-in-class. The U.S. pathway is the dossier's largest single unknown: no NDA has been filed, no timeline disclosed, and the company is spending on a U.S. commercial build as if a filing follows PREVAIL — without saying so.

12 / RISKS

Ranked red flags

Ordered by capacity to impair the thesis, not by probability.

  • PREVAIL is the companyA futility stop or a final HR near 1.0 (the evacetrapib outcome) leaves an EU royalty stream worth a fraction of the current ~$1.88B EV. No pipeline item behind it can replace it.
  • The interim can "succeed" and still disappointThe modal positive path — no early stop, trial continues — delivers no filing, no launch, and another year of burn before the next catalyst. A stock priced for a binary does not reward a deferral.
  • U.S. regulatory path is undisclosedNo NDA, no timeline, no disclosed FDA meeting outcome. If FDA requires the completed CVOT for any approval, the U.S. revenue start is 2028 at the earliest — against an enlicitide clock.
  • Enlicitide (Merck oral PCSK9)A potent oral from a sponsor with outcomes-trial infrastructure and primary-care reach — already FDA-approved (LIPFENDRA, Jul 2026) with −56% to −59% placebo-adjusted LDL-C, deeper than obicetrapib monotherapy cross-trial, and launching in the U.S. while obicetrapib is unfiled. Only Lp(a) and the FDC remain as differentiation.
  • Europe is a royalty, at partner paceNewAmsterdam does not control launch sequencing, pricing negotiations, or salesforce effort. EU lipid launches are historically slow, country-staggered, and reimbursement-gated; the €833M milestone tail is mostly commercial and back-ended.
  • Class-history residue in the labelThe BROADWAY eGFR mean-change signal, plus the class's accumulation precedent, invite conservative labeling and post-marketing requirements that can cap the addressable population if PREVAIL succeeds.
  • Dilution is structural, not episodic19.9M options at a $16.91 average strike — 16.9% of basic shares — plus RSUs, pre-funded warrants and residual SPAC warrants. Every scenario above already pays this tax via the treasury method; headline per-share upside that ignores it is overstated.
  • Single-molecule concentration with an expensive side-questSPINOZA (Alzheimer's) and REMBRANDT consume cash and management attention while the core asset is mid-binary. If PREVAIL fails, the AD biomarker story will not support the market cap.
  • FX and domicile frictionA Dutch N.V. earning euro royalties, spending dollars, reporting in USD — with a warrant line and a €0.12-par share structure inherited from the SPAC. None of it is disqualifying; all of it adds noise to per-share math that casual screens get wrong.
