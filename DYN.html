03 / SCIENCE

FORCE: an antibody taxi for oligonucleotides that muscle otherwise refuses

Unconjugated oligonucleotides barely enter skeletal muscle — the reason Sarepta's PMOs are dosed at 30–80 mg/kg weekly and still produce ~1% dystrophin. Dyne's bet is that transferrin receptor 1 (TfR1) targeting changes the delivery math by an order of magnitude.

01

TfR1 binding

An antigen-binding fragment (Fab) against transferrin receptor 1 — highly expressed on skeletal and cardiac muscle — carries the payload across the muscle membrane via receptor-mediated uptake.

02

Conjugated payload

A clinically validated linker joins the Fab to the oligo: a PMO for exon skipping (DMD) or an ASO against DMPK RNA (DM1). Conjugation, not encapsulation — one defined molecule.

03

Nuclear action

In DMD, the PMO skips exon 51 in pre-mRNA, restoring the reading frame so a shortened, Becker-like dystrophin is made. In DM1, the ASO reduces toxic nuclear DMPK RNA, releasing sequestered splicing proteins (MBNL).

04

Functional claim

Higher muscle exposure at lower dose: z-rostudirsen runs at 20 mg/kg every 4 weeks vs weekly unconjugated PMO; z-basivarsen at 6.8 mg/kg every 8 weeks. Delivery also reaches the CNS at preclinical level, relevant to DM1's brain manifestations.

FORCE in 60 seconds INTERPRETATION

Plain English. Muscle is a fortress for RNA drugs: almost none of an injected dose gets in. Dyne bolts each oligo to half an antibody that grabs a handle (TfR1) muscle cells use to import iron — the cell pulls the whole package inside. The oligo then fixes the message: skip the broken exon in DMD, or destroy the toxic message that jams splicing in DM1.

Technical. FORCE = Fab (anti-TfR1) + linker + oligonucleotide payload, administered IV. TfR1 is also expressed on reticulocytes and other proliferating cells, so the same handle that enables muscle delivery creates the platform's core safety questions: chronic receptor engagement, iron/erythropoiesis biology, and immunogenicity against both Fab and payload.

1 · Delivery does not scale with dose

TfR1-mediated uptake can saturate, and biopsy-measured dystrophin comes from one muscle at one timepoint. If human muscle exposure plateaus below the functional threshold, the entire premium over unconjugated PMOs collapses — Sarepta's drugs are the control case for "some dystrophin, limited function."

2 · Immunogenicity

Repeat IV dosing of a Fab-oligo conjugate invites anti-drug antibodies against the Fab, the linker, or the oligo — with loss of exposure or infusion reactions over the multi-year dosing these diseases require. Long-term ADA data remain limited to small cohorts.

3 · TfR1 and ASO class effects

TfR1 sits on erythroid precursors; sustained engagement is a theoretical anemia/iron risk that needs chronic monitoring. ASOs as a class carry hepatic, renal, platelet, and complement signals (the drisapersen and inotersen precedents); PMOs have been cleaner, but the conjugate is a new molecular entity, not a PMO.

4 · Splicing correction ≠ function

DM1's spliceopathy is broad; correcting a composite splicing index does not guarantee clinically meaningful change in strength or function — HARBOR showed clinical activity on secondaries yet failed its functional primary. Molecular response is necessary, not sufficient.

Preclinical anchor

Conjugation raised dystrophin where PMO alone could not

FACT In the mdx mouse model, a single IV dose of a TfR1-Fab–PMO conjugate produced robust, durable exon skipping in cardiac and skeletal muscle; in non-human primates, z-rostudirsen achieved robust exon skipping in heart and diaphragm with a favorable safety profile (FY2025 10-K). Heart and diaphragm delivery is the scientifically important claim — respiratory and cardiac failure drive DMD mortality, and unconjugated PMOs penetrate both poorly.

The honest gap

Human target-engagement proof is biopsy math

GAP There is no direct human measurement of muscle oligo concentration at the registrational doses; the chain from dose → muscle exposure → dystrophin/splicing → function is inferred from biopsies (one muscle, ~28 days post-dose) and downstream biomarkers. FDA's review of the BLA is, in large part, a review of that inference chain.

Platform and preclinical claims: FY2025 10-K. Class context: Roberts, Langer & Wood, Nat Rev Drug Discov 2020 (PMID 32782413) · Mendell et al., Ann Neurol 2016 (eteplirsen dystrophin).

05 / REGULATORY RISK

Two regulatory overhangs: a damaged surrogate pathway, and a failed endpoint twin

Neither overhang is Dyne's making. Both will be in the room when FDA decides the BLA in January and reads the REC in Q1.

FACT

The EleVidys precedent (DMD)

  • Jun. 2025: FDA opened a safety investigation after two fatal acute liver failures in non-ambulatory boys treated with EleVidys (Sarepta's AAV gene therapy); Sarepta paused non-ambulatory shipments.
  • Nov. 14, 2025: FDA revised the EleVidys label — boxed warning for acute serious liver injury/liver failure, indication limited to ambulatory patients ≥4 years, non-ambulatory use removed, postmarketing liver-safety study required.
  • The accelerated-approval architecture itself survived: EleVidys kept its ambulatory approval and Sarepta's three PMOs (all dystrophin-surrogate approvals) were untouched.
  • Jul. 20, 2026: FDA accepted Dyne's BLA and granted Priority Review with a Jan. 21, 2027 PDUFA — a post-crisis, eyes-open acceptance of a dystrophin-based filing.
FACT

The HARBOR precedent (DM1)

  • Sept. 8, 2026: Novartis announced Phase 3 HARBOR (n=159) did not meet its primary endpoint of vHOT for del-desiran — a TfR1 antibody-oligonucleotide conjugate from the Avidity acquisition — despite clinical activity on secondary/exploratory measures.
  • vHOT is the primary endpoint of Dyne's ACHIEVE REC (n=71) and the proposed intermediate clinical endpoint for z-basivarsen's accelerated approval.
  • Structural differences cut both ways: del-desiran is an siRNA degrader payload on a full antibody; z-basivarsen is an ASO on a Fab, with demonstrated CASI-22 splicing correction — HARBOR disclosed no splicing data, so a molecular-vs-endpoint failure cannot be separated from outside.
  • Dyne's own confirmatory trial (HARMONIA) does not use vHOT as primary — it uses 5xSTS — which limits, but does not eliminate, the read-across.
What FDA has actually signaled. INTERPRETATION The observable signals are behavioral, not rhetorical: (1) the agency narrowed EleVidys on safety while leaving the surrogate pathway standing; (2) it granted Priority Review to a dystrophin BLA filed after the crisis; (3) it aligned with Dyne on two confirmatory designs (FORZETTO: RFF velocity; HARMONIA: 5xSTS) that use functional primaries. The residual CRL risk concentrates in three places: the muscle-content-adjustment methodology behind the 5.46% figure, CMC for a first-of-kind conjugate at commercial scale, and whether a 32-patient REC plus small MAD cohorts is an adequate safety database for chronic dosing. None of these is handicappable from public data — they are the honest content of the January binary. GAP No advisory committee has been announced for the BLA as of Oct. 20, 2026; whether one is convened is itself a signal to watch in Q4.

12 / RISKS

Ranked red flags

Ranked by expected damage to the equity story, not by probability alone.

  1. Correlated binary concentration~$1.93B of EV rests on two verdicts in one quarter that share a root cause — FDA's posture toward novel endpoints and surrogates. They are not independent coin flips; a bad January plausibly bleeds into the REC read.
  2. vHOT is a demonstrated Phase 3 failure riskHARBOR (n=159, same modality class) missed vHOT in Phase 3. Dyne's DM1 accelerated-approval plan asks FDA to accept that same measure as an intermediate endpoint on n=71 Phase 1/2 data.
  3. Surrogate methodology scrutinyThe 5.46% dystrophin figure is muscle-content-adjusted, from one biopsy site ~28 days post-dose, in 24 treated patients. Adjustment methodology and biopsy variability are classic CRL territory even when the headline number is large.
  4. Functional evidence is nominalDELIVER's functional separation rests on post-hoc, pooled-placebo analyses the company itself describes as not powered and not prespecified for hypothesis testing. An approval can still issue — but a skeptical review division has material to work with.
  5. Burn and dilution trajectoryH1 burn $275M and rising; two confirmatory Phase 3s plus a launch to fund; +83% shares in 19 months; $200M Hercules debt with an end-of-term charge. The Q2 2028 runway has no room for a CRL cycle.
  6. Small-n, pooled-analysis evidence baseRegistrational-dose cohorts of n=24 (DMD) and n=6 (DM1 12-month data); the headline DM1 one-year analysis pools three dose levels with crossover re-baselining. Effect sizes may not replicate at REC scale.
  7. Platform class safety (TfR1 / ASO)Chronic TfR1 engagement (erythroid/iron biology), anti-drug antibodies, and ASO-class hepatic/platelet/complement signals are unquantified at registrational exposure. Any one surfacing in review or post-marketing hits both assets at once.
  8. Funded competition on both flanksSarepta's installed DMD franchise (~$966M PMO revenue) defends exon 51 commercially; Novartis's $12B AOC platform contests DMD44 and DM1 with big-pharma resources, and its del-zota filing competes for the same FDA review bandwidth.
13 / OPEN

What would change the answer

Disclosed gaps, stated rather than filled.

GAP 01

AdCom or not?

No advisory committee for the z-rostudirsen BLA has been announced as of Oct. 20, 2026. A convened AdCom would raise January variance; its continued absence into December is mild positive signal.

GAP 02

Unadjusted dystrophin & biopsy detail

The public record leads with the muscle-content-adjusted 5.46%. The full biopsy dataset (per-patient values, unadjusted levels, sampling variance) sits in the BLA, not in public filings.

GAP 03

Why HARBOR missed

Novartis has not disclosed del-desiran's splicing/DMPK-knockdown data. Whether HARBOR was a molecular failure or an endpoint failure is the single most decision-relevant unknown for the REC — and it is currently unknowable.

GAP 04

Q3 2026 actuals

Pro-forma cash (~$1,303.5M) is an estimate built from the Jun. 30 balance plus offering proceeds. Q3 results (expected November) will show the true post-raise balance and the burn step-up.

GAP 05

Label scope

The BLA seeks treatment of DMD "amenable to exon 51 skipping"; DELIVER enrolled ambulant and non-ambulant patients. Post-EleVidys, whether FDA writes the label across both populations is open.

GAP 06

CMC at commercial scale

First-of-kind Fab-oligo conjugate manufacturing (oligo synthesis + conjugation + PPQ batches) is referenced in expense lines but not described in public detail. CMC is a silent CRL cause across the industry.

INTERPRETATION

The dossier's verdict

Dyne is the rare platform story whose pivotal numbers are large enough to survive honest discounting: 5.46% dystrophin is not 0.9%, and a 3-plus-second vHOT separation is not noise — in cohorts of 24 and 6. That tension is the whole position. The January PDUFA is a methodology review dressed as a drug review, and the Q1 REC is an endpoint review dressed as a data readout. At ~$1.93B of enterprise value with ~$1.10B of net cash beneath it, the market is paying for the DMD asset and carrying DM1 cheaply; the bear case is not "zero," it is "cash plus a wounded platform," and it is reachable in a single quarter. Everything that matters resolves by the end of Q1 2027.
