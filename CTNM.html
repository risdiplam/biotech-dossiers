03 / SCIENCE

LPA1: a fibroblast-recruitment switch with human genetics-free, knockout-grade preclinical support — and a liver shadow

Plain English first, then the technical chain, then why it might fail.

Plain English

In IPF, the lung repeatedly injures and mis-repairs. A fat-like signal called LPA accumulates in the injured lung and, through its LPA1 receptor, calls in scar-forming fibroblasts, leaks blood vessels, and helps kill surface (epithelial) cells — three steps that produce scar instead of air sacs. PIPE-791 is a once-daily pill that blocks LPA1, aiming to slow the scarring process rather than reverse scar already laid down. The realistic best outcome, as with every approved IPF drug, is slower loss of lung capacity (FVC), not recovery.

PIPE-307, the partnered asset, works in the brain: blocking the M1 muscarinic receptor was meant to release a brake on myelin-making cells (remyelination in MS) and to modulate circuits in depression. It has now failed an efficacy test in each indication.

01

LPA rises in IPF lung

LPA is elevated in bronchoalveolar lavage fluid from IPF patients, and IPF lavage fluid drives fibroblast chemotaxis that an LPA1 blocker abolishes (Tager et al., Nat Med 2008). FACT

02

LPA1 drives fibrosis steps

LPA1 mediates fibroblast recruitment, vascular leak and epithelial apoptosis after lung injury (Tager 2008; Funke et al. 2012). FACT

03

Knockout protection

LPA1-deficient mice are protected from bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis, with less fibroblast recruitment and vascular leak (Tager 2008); an oral LPA1 antagonist reproduced benefit in the same model (Swaney et al., Br J Pharmacol 2010). FACT

04

PIPE-791's claimed edge

A distinct scaffold giving slow, tight LPA1 binding, brain penetration and full occupancy at a low once-daily dose, per its discovery paper (Chen et al., J Med Chem, Jun 29, 2026). Chemistry/preclinical evidence — not clinical efficacy. FACT

Human target engagement

PET occupancy: engagement shown, in brain — not yet in IPF lung at Phase 2 doses

In a Phase 1b PET study (reported Sep 18, 2025), PIPE-791 achieved high brain LPA1 receptor occupancy in healthy volunteers and progressive MS patients, with a PK–occupancy relationship (plasma EC50 37 ng/mL at 24 h; 12 ng/mL at 168 h in healthy volunteers). The company states planned Phase 2 doses should exceed 90% target coverage at trough on once-daily dosing. FACT Two qualifications matter: occupancy was demonstrated in brain, while PROPEL-IPF's target organ is lung; and ALOFT has just shown that more occupancy in this class did not translate into more FVC benefit at the higher dose — so occupancy is a necessary, not sufficient, argument. INTERPRETATION

Why it might fail

1 · Bleomycin ≠ human IPF

The knockout/antagonist evidence rests heavily on the mouse bleomycin model, an injury-driven, partly self-resolving system that has predicted many IPF failures. Human validation arrived only on Oct 12, 2026 — at one of two doses, with no effect sizes disclosed. FACT INTERPRETATION

2 · The dose–response paradox

Admilparant 60 mg BID met its endpoint; 120 mg BID did not. Proposed explanations (placebo-arm noise, exposure-related tolerability/adherence, a genuine bell-shaped response) cannot be distinguished without the full dataset. If the paradox is mechanistic, PIPE-791's high-occupancy strategy inherits it. GAP

3 · Hepatobiliary class history

BMS-986020, BMS's first-generation LPA1 antagonist, showed a Phase 2 FVC signal but was halted for off-target hepatobiliary toxicity (bile-acid transporter inhibition, per the admilparant literature). In 2026, ALOFT reported a limited number of liver injury events including one death in a blinded program, a May protocol amendment and a dedicated liver-injury committee; the DMC recommended continuation. Causality — molecule, dose, mechanism, or background — is unresolved. FACT GAP

4 · Slowing decline is a small-signal game

Approved agents shift 52-week FVC by roughly 70–130 mL against noisy, variable decline. PROPEL-IPF is 26 weeks and ~324 patients — powered, presumably, on an admilparant-like effect; a smaller true effect, background-therapy mix, or site heterogeneity can sink it. Powering assumptions are not public. GAP

5 · M1 antagonism has now missed twice

The remyelination rationale (M1 blockade releasing oligodendrocyte-progenitor differentiation; Poon et al. 2024) failed to move LCLA or any secondary endpoint in VISTA, joining opicinumab and clemastine-era translational disappointments; the rapid-onset depression rationale failed at Day 5 on MADRS in MOONLIGHT-1. Two independent misses lower the prior for a third indication rescue. FACT INTERPRETATION

6 · Brain penetration is double-edged in IPF

PIPE-791's CNS penetration is essential for MS/pain optionality but unnecessary exposure for an elderly, comorbid IPF population; any CNS or drug-interaction signal in PROPEL-IPF would be a self-inflicted liability its peripherally-restricted sibling (CTX-343) was designed to avoid. INTERPRETATION

PIPE-307 (JNJ-5120) · RRMS · Phase 2 · Failed

VISTA (NCT06083753) — topline Nov 20, 2025

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled proof-of-concept; N=168 RRMS patients, two PIPE-307 doses vs placebo. Primary endpoint — change in binocular 2.5% low-contrast letter acuity (LCLA) — was not met; no prespecified secondary efficacy endpoint was met; no significant LCLA change across arms. Safety/tolerability acceptable at both doses. No effect sizes or p-values have been published; full dataset presentation/publication was promised and has not appeared in the materials reviewed. Contineum self-funded this trial under its J&J license election; with its completion, further MS development is at J&J's sole discretion. FACT GAP

PIPE-307 (JNJ-5120) · MDD · Phase 2 · Failed

MOONLIGHT-1 (NCT06785012) — topline Sep 14, 2026

J&J-run randomized, double-blind, multicenter, placebo-controlled proof-of-concept; N=107 adults with major depressive disorder (enrollment completed Jun 2026), two JNJ-5120 regimens vs placebo, monotherapy. Primary endpoint — change in MADRS at Day 5 vs placebo — was not met. Well tolerated, no new safety signals. J&J is evaluating prespecified exploratory endpoints; whether development continues is undisclosed. No effect size or p-value disclosed. FACT GAP Note the endpoint design: a Day-5 MADRS bar is a rapid-onset claim, an unusually hard test — the miss kills the rapid-onset story specifically, and any slower-onset hypothesis now depends entirely on J&J's exploratory read and willingness to fund another trial. INTERPRETATION

Regulatory path — what is actually known

No PIPE-791 regulatory designation (Breakthrough, Fast Track, Orphan) is disclosed in the materials reviewed. GAP The standard IPF path applies: PROPEL-IPF is a Phase 2 dose-finding study; a positive result would still require a 52-week Phase 3 (the ALOFT/FIBRONEER precedent) before an NDA — i.e., no filing before ~2030 on any realistic timeline. INTERPRETATION Admilparant's path is the live template: BMS states it will discuss submissions with regulators after full ALOFT analysis; if a single-dose Phase 3 win earns a filing, it also sets the efficacy bar and the label language PIPE-791 will eventually be judged against. FACT

05 / LANDSCAPE

Clinical differentiation, not marketing: PIPE-791 is third in its own class queue

Three oral antifibrotics are approved; one LPA1 antagonist is Phase 3-positive; one deuterated pirfenidone is Phase 3-bound with 26-week FVC data stronger than the class norm. Cross-trial comparisons are flagged as such — populations, background therapy and durations differ, and no head-to-head data exist.

Ranked red flags

Ordered by capacity to destroy the thesis, not by probability.

  1. ALOFT's missing dose–response is mechanistic until proven otherwiseIf LPA1 occupancy does not scale to FVC benefit, PIPE-791's core design premise (>90% trough occupancy) is aimed at the wrong variable, and PROPEL-IPF's two undisclosed doses are a coin-flip on an unknown exposure–response curve. Full ALOFT data is the only resolution. GAP

If the eventual unblinded table attributes injury to admilparant, regulators will import that scrutiny into PROPEL-IPF mid-trial — monitoring, eligibility, or worse. FACT

12 / OPEN QUESTIONS

The evidence that would change the view

UPGRADE

ALOFT full data exculpates the mechanism

Published effect sizes showing a coherent exposure story (e.g., a documented adherence/tolerability explanation for 120 mg) plus a liver table that is placebo-like on drug — that combination would materially raise PROPEL-IPF's prior and re-rate the EV off $61M.

UPGRADE

PROPEL-IPF dose disclosure + enrollment pace

Publication of the Dose A/B amounts with lung (not just brain) occupancy support, and an enrollment-completion guide that holds Jun 2028, would convert the two biggest GAPs in this dossier into facts.

DOWNGRADE

Unblinded ALOFT liver attribution

If the fatal liver injury and the event cluster sit on admilparant arms at rates above placebo, expect FDA/DSMB attention to PROPEL-IPF and a further class de-rating regardless of PIPE-791's different scaffold.

DOWNGRADE

ATM use at these prices

Any material sale under the $100M ATM below ~$8 would signal management itself does not believe the mid-2029 runway narrative — or sees a burn the guide has not admitted.

WATCH

J&J's PIPE-307 decision

Termination closes the file at zero; a funded new trial (any indication) would be a genuine, if small, positive surprise — it is carried at ~zero in every scenario here.

WATCH

Insider buying at ~cash value

The first open-market purchase by any officer/director at the $7–8 level would be the cheapest credibility signal available to this management team. Its continued absence is data.

Verdict

A validated class, an unvalidated molecule, and ~$61M of enterprise value for a 2028 answer.

CTNM is no longer the two-asset NI&I story it IPO'd as — it is a cash-rich, single-binary LPA1 option whose mechanism was de-risked and whose differentiation was complicated by the same Oct 12, 2026 readout. The balance sheet genuinely funds the binary, which is rarer than it should be at this market cap. What the dossier cannot resolve — ALOFT's effect sizes, its liver attribution, PROPEL-IPF's doses and powering — is exactly what the next twelve months of other people's disclosures will resolve for free. Position sizing, as ever, is the reader's call, not this document's.
