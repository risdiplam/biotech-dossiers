CRB-913 in four steps

01

Brain CB1

FACT · LITERATURE Central CB1 signaling participates in appetite and reward. That is why rimonabant reduced weight—and why central psychiatric liability matters.

02

Peripheral CB1

FACT · PRECLINICAL CB1 in adipose, liver and gut is implicated in lipogenesis, storage and metabolic dysfunction; this is the peripheral thesis.

03

Restriction

INTERPRETATION Limiting brain exposure aims to preserve metabolic efficacy while avoiding central psychiatric AEs.

04

Inverse agonism

FACT · PHARMACOLOGY CRB-913 is described as an oral, once-daily CB1 inverse agonist that reduces constitutive receptor signaling.

Why the class fails

Psychiatric safety is the burden of proof

FACT · PEER-REVIEWED A 2007 Lancet meta-analysis found increased depressed-mood and anxiety-related discontinuation with 20 mg/day rimonabant despite psychiatric exclusions; the drug was withdrawn in Europe in 2008.

FACT · PEER-REVIEWED In a 16-week Phase 2a study, monlunabant produced meaningful weight loss but 13–42% AE withdrawals and 28–42% psychiatric AEs across doses. INTERPRETATION Peripheral CB1 must win on efficacy and tolerability simultaneously.

UNVERIFIED · MOUSE Corbus’s “15× lower brain penetration than monlunabant” comparison is a mouse claim and does not establish human brain exposure.

Biologic plausibility

JD5037 validates the concept—in mice

FACT · PEER-REVIEWED Tam et al. reported that peripheral CB1 inverse agonism with JD5037 reduced obesity and reversed leptin resistance in diet-induced obese mice (Cell Metabolism 2012;16:167–179).

INTERPRETATION This supports the mechanism, not CRB-913’s human therapeutic index.

FACT · PEER-REVIEWED Wadden et al. found no increased depression or suicidality versus placebo with semaglutide 2.4 mg in pooled STEP 1/2/3 and STEP 5 participants without major psychopathology (JAMA Internal Medicine 2024;184:1290–1300).

GAP Corbus has not disclosed C-SSRS, PHQ-9 or GAD-7 scale results from CANYON-1.

Other oral programs raise the bar

FACT Oral amycretin (GLP-1/amylin) moved toward Phase 3 development after early studies; oral VK2735 (GLP-1/GIP) reported up to 12.2% mean baseline weight loss at Week 13 in Phase 2. INTERPRETATION Those programs reinforce that convenience alone is not differentiation.

Monlunabant remains the clean benchmark

FACT · PEER-REVIEWED Knop et al., Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology 2025;13:911–923, randomized 243 adults with obesity and metabolic syndrome across placebo and 10/20/50 mg doses for 16 weeks. INTERPRETATION CRB-913’s principal claim is not “CB1 causes weight loss”; it is “CB1 can be blocked without unacceptable CNS toxicity.”

Second leg · CRB-701

Nectin-4 ADC competition is crowded

FACT Padcev is established with a boxed warning; Mabwell’s 9MW2821 is in China Phase 3 programs including cervical cancer; petosemtamab has FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation with pembrolizumab in first-line PD-L1-positive HNSCC; tisotumab vedotin is a second-line cervical cancer standard.

Read-through

Not a free hedge

FACT · COMPANY DATA CRB-701’s registrational thesis in OPSCC rests on a single-arm n=21 subgroup with 42.9% confirmed ORR; any ocular AE occurred in 66.2%, keratitis in 49.2% and Grade 3 ocular AEs in 12.6%. INTERPRETATION The ADC may shift toxicity rather than eliminate it. FACT · COMPANY PR (MAY 26, 2026) Confirmed ORR in 2L cervical cancer was 34.4% (11/32) at 3.6 mg/kg, with median DoR 8.0 months.
