02 / SCIENCE

Fine-tuning the sarcomere, not shutting it down

HCM is a disease of a heart that contracts too hard and relaxes too slowly. Every approved or late-stage drug in the class reduces contractile force. Edgewise's claim is that its molecules improve relaxation preferentially — helping diastole while sparing systole.

01

Sarcomere hypercontractility

Pathogenic variants (commonly MYH7, MYBPC3) and acquired remodeling increase myosin cross-bridge formation, raising energy cost, wall stress and outflow obstruction in the obstructive phenotype. FACT

02

Diastolic dysfunction

Impaired relaxation and increased stiffness raise filling pressures — the shared physiology of nonobstructive HCM and much of HFpEF, and the driver of breathlessness and exercise intolerance. FACT

03

The class trade-off

Cardiac myosin inhibitors (mavacamten, aficamten) reduce cross-bridges and gradients, but dose is limited by falling LVEF and systolic heart-failure risk — hence echo-guided titration and, for mavacamten, a boxed warning and REMS. Meta-analysis of randomized trials confirms efficacy on gradients, NYHA and KCCQ with a modest mean LVEF decrease. FACT

04

Edgewise's proposed break

EDG-7500 is described by the company as a selective cardiac sarcomere modulator acting via the myosin regulatory light chain, slowing early contraction velocity and speeding relaxation while preserving myosin availability and net systolic function. UNVERIFIED · COMPANY MECHANISM DISCLOSURE, SEP 22, 2026

The mechanism in 60 seconds

Plain English. Think of each heartbeat as a squeeze and a release. In HCM the squeeze is too strong and the release is too slow, so the heart cannot fill properly. Existing drugs loosen the squeeze — effective, but loosen it too much and the pump weakens. Edgewise aims to speed up the release instead, so the heart fills better without a weaker pump.

Technical. EDG-7500 and EDG-15400 are oral, selective cardiac sarcomere modulators. Per company disclosure (Sep 2026), EDG-7500 targets the regulatory light chain axis rather than the myosin ATPase targeted by mavacamten/aficamten, reducing early systolic contraction velocity and increasing early diastolic relaxation velocity (e′), with no observed concentration–LVEF relationship in clinical echocardiography to date. Independent peer-reviewed characterization of EDG-7500's target engagement in humans has not been published. GAP

1 · Open-label mirage

KCCQ gains of +24 / +13 points are far larger than placebo-adjusted gains seen in blinded CMI trials. Without a control arm, expectation, regression to the mean and titration attention can manufacture most of that signal.

2 · LVEF liability emerges at scale

The class effect is dose- and exposure-related. ACACIA-HCM showed LVEF <50% in 10.5% on aficamten under forced titration to maximum tolerated dose. A Phase 3 that titrates harder for efficacy may find the same wall.

3 · Diastole is not the registrational endpoint

Regulators approve on symptoms/function (KCCQ, pVO2, NYHA composites), not e′ velocity. Mavacamten improved hemodynamics in nonobstructive HCM yet missed its dual primary endpoints in ODYSSEY-HCM — biomarker movement did not convert.

4 · HFpEF heterogeneity

HFpEF is dozens of diseases sharing an ejection fraction. A relaxation drug can work physiologically and still fail on outcomes in an unselected population — the pattern of two decades of HFpEF trials.

Literature: Olivotto et al., EXPLORER-HCM, Lancet 2020 (mavacamten Phase 3) · Maron et al., SEQUOIA-HCM, NEJM 2024 (aficamten) · Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine meta-analysis of cardiac myosin inhibitor RCTs (2024) · ACACIA-HCM results, ESC / NEJM Aug 2026 and HFSA Oct 2026 disclosures · ODYSSEY-HCM topline, BMS 2025.

10 / RISKS

Ranked red flags

Ordered by capacity to destroy the thesis, not by probability.

  1. Open-label evidence baseEvery efficacy claim for the lead asset rests on uncontrolled cohorts (largest n=53, 12 weeks). Blinded shrinkage is the base rate in this class, not the exception.
  2. Third-mover commercial positionMavacamten (~$1.07B 2025 sales, pediatric label) and aficamten (approved oHCM, Phase 3-positive nHCM) will have years of prescriber habit, payer contracts and real-world data before EDG-7500 can launch.
  3. Class LVEF liability may be dose-inseparableACACIA quantified it for aficamten in nHCM (LVEF <50%: 10.5% vs 0.8%). If relaxation and systolic depression are the same pharmacology at Phase 3 exposures, EDG-7500's differentiation collapses.
  4. Cash is gross, not netThe $2.01B figure is pre-tax, pre-cost, pre-Q3-burn arithmetic. Anyone underwriting $18.66/share of cash without the Q3 10-Q is underwriting a number no filing states.
  5. Milestones are someone else's execution$1.1B of headline value depends on Servier's development, regulatory and commercial performance — with approval triggers, not readout triggers, and an adult-only Becker label halving the first payment to $100M.
  6. HFpEF prior probabilityEDG-15400 enters the indication with the worst translation record in cardiology, at Phase 2, n≈90, 12 weeks — surrogate-scale evidence at best from this study.
  7. Price already pays for success~$2.1B of operating EV for a company whose lead asset has never faced a placebo. The June 2026 pattern — stock down ~25% intraday on positive Part D data (trade summaries) — shows how much was already in the price.
  8. Key-person / transitionThe muscular-dystrophy team largely moved to Servier; the cardiovascular bench that must now execute a pivotal program is thinner in the public record. GAP
