03 / SCIENCE

Notch blockade in a β-catenin disease — effective, and toxic for the same reason

Desmoid tumors are driven by Wnt/β-catenin signaling (85–90% sporadic, ~85% of those carrying CTNNB1 mutations). Gamma-secretase inhibition does not touch β-catenin directly; it blocks Notch receptor cleavage, and Notch–Wnt crosstalk is part of what sustains desmoid growth. The same Notch dependence in gut, skin, and ovarian follicles produces the class's signature toxicities. Efficacy and the side-effect ceiling are mechanistically inseparable — that is the whole science story of this drug class.

01

The tumor

Locally aggressive, non-metastasizing fibroblastic neoplasm. 1,000–1,650 new US diagnoses per year (company 10-K figure); recurrence after surgery 60–80%, which is why management shifted to active surveillance first, systemic therapy on progression. FACT

02

The driver

Stabilized β-catenin (CTNNB1 or APC loss) accumulates in the nucleus and drives proliferative gene programs. FACT

03

The lever

Gamma-secretase cleaves Notch receptors, releasing the Notch intracellular domain (NICD). Varegacestat inhibits the enzyme, cutting Notch signaling that desmoid cells co-opt alongside Wnt. FACT

04

The cost

Notch is also required for intestinal goblet-cell differentiation, skin homeostasis, and ovarian folliculogenesis — hence diarrhea, rash, and ovarian toxicity across every GSI ever dosed chronically. FACT

Why gamma-secretase has a graveyard — and why oncology dosing is different

The class's defining failure was Alzheimer's disease: Lilly's semagacestat not only missed in Phase 3 (IDENTITY, halted 2010) but worsened cognition, because chronic systemic Notch/APP-cleavage blockade at CNS-intent doses is intolerable — with excess skin cancers and infections on top. BMS's avagacestat was discontinued after Phase 2 for similar tolerability/dose reasons. (Fittingly, varegacestat itself began life inside BMS as a licensed compound before passing to Ayala.) The oncology use case survives because the therapeutic index question is different: a progressing tumor with no good options, a younger but treatment-needing population, and benefit measured in months of progression-free survival rather than decades of prevention. The toxicities did not disappear — they were repriced. Any reader who sees "GSI" and stops at the Alzheimer's history is missing that repricing; any reader who ignores the history misses why FDA will read the ovarian and skin data line by line.

INTERPRETATION The relevant scientific risk is not the Alzheimer's precedent. It is duration: desmoid therapy is chronic (median exposure 20.3 months in RINGSIDE), cumulative Notch toxicity in a young, often female population — ovarian function above all — is the long-horizon unknown no 2-year trial fully resolves.

Failure mode 1 — ovarian toxicity proves dose-limiting in practice

56% incidence in premenopausal women (RINGSIDE), resolution in 55% at cut. If real-world discontinuation for ovarian effects runs hotter than the trial's zero, the drug's usable population shrinks toward post-menopausal and male patients — a fraction of a 1,000–1,650/yr incidence market.

Failure mode 2 — the 80% dose-reduction rate is the real dose

Four of five patients reduced dose for AEs. If the delivered dose intensity in practice drifts below the trial's managed level, the ORR/PFS package may not reproduce — while the label will still carry the full-dose efficacy claims.

Failure mode 3 — second-mover entrenchment

Ogsiveo's two-year head start, guideline inclusion, and Merck KGaA's sales infrastructure may make "good enough and familiar" beat "once-daily and somewhat cleaner" in sarcoma centers.

Failure mode 4 — the platform is a call option, not a pipeline

IM-1021, IM-1617 and IM-3050 are all Phase 1, first-in-human in 2025–2026. ADC and radioligand fields are littered with Phase 1 responses that never became drugs; ROR1 specifically has burned sponsors before (see Pipeline).

Disease background and epidemiology: FY2025 10-K, Business section. Class history: published Phase 3 record of semagacestat (IDENTITY program) and the OGSIVEO label's class warnings (Landscape sources).

04 / CLINICAL

RINGSIDE: the strongest desmoid dataset ever produced — with an 80% asterisk

RINGSIDE (NCT04871282) is a single seamless Phase 2/3 study Ayala designed and Immunome inherited. Part A picked the dose; Part B was the registrational, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Part B enrolled 156 patients with progressing desmoid tumors (≥20% growth in the prior 12 months), randomized 1:1 to varegacestat 1.2 mg once daily or placebo, with progression assessed by blinded independent central review.

Ovarian toxicity — the label fight

FACT 20 of 36 premenopausal women (56%) had ovarian-toxicity AEs; resolved in 11 (55%) at cut; zero discontinuations for ovarian toxicity. Compare the OGSIVEO label: 75% of 36 females of reproductive potential, with ovarian toxicity among its serious reactions and a labeled warning. Cross-trial context only — populations and ascertainment differ, and no head-to-head exists. This is varegacestat's best differentiation datapoint and it is still a class-effect coin flip at the individual patient level.

Dose reductions — the asterisk

FACT Dose reductions for TEAEs: 80% on varegacestat vs 9% on placebo. AE discontinuations: 20% vs 7%. Grade 3/4 AEs in 57% vs 17% of patients (95% of all events were grade 1–2). Median exposure 20.3 vs 11.1 months, no treatment-related deaths. Against DeFi's label figures for Ogsiveo (reductions 42%, interruptions 51%, discontinuations 20%), varegacestat's delivered-dose profile is worse on reductions and equal on discontinuations. Tolerability differentiation is a dosing-schedule and ovarian argument, not a blanket claim.

The common-AE background

FACT Diarrhea 82% (vs 27% pbo), fatigue 44% (23%), rash 43% (12%), nausea 35% (26%), cough 34% (5%). This is the GSI signature — manageable, chronic, quality-of-life-relevant in a young population taking the drug for ~20 months. It caps how "clean" any GSI label can honestly be.

Dose selection · Phase 2 (RINGSIDE Part A)

Why 1.2 mg once daily

Part A enrolled 42 participants across three regimens — 1.2 mg once daily, 2 mg twice-weekly, 4 mg twice-weekly. ORR in evaluable patients was 64% across doses and 75% at 1.2 mg QD (ITT: 64% at 1.2 mg QD; per-arm ASCO 2023 evaluable ORRs: 50% QD at first cut, rising with follow-up; 45.5% at 2 mg BIW; 23.1% at 4 mg BIW). The daily regimen also produced the fastest and deepest tumor-volume and T2-weighted MRI signal reductions (−88% median best volume change). Dose selection was efficacy-led, and Part B validated it. FACT (10-K; Ayala ASCO/ESMO 2023 presentations)

Registry: NCT04871282 (actual enrollment 198 across Parts A+B+OLE; status active, not recruiting). Topline: Business Wire, Dec 15, 2025.

06 / PIPELINE

One near-commercial asset and three Phase 1 lottery tickets, ranked honestly

Immunome's post-2023 identity is Siegall's ADC playbook rebuilt: a discovery engine producing TOP1-inhibitor ADCs (proprietary HC74 payload) plus a FAP radioligand inherited from Morphimmune. The pipeline beyond varegacestat is real but early — all of it is Phase 1 or pre-IND, and none of it should carry much of the current EV in a disciplined model.

Ranked red flags

Ranked by expected damage to the dossier's base case, not by headline drama. The top three are all the same risk wearing different clothes: varegacestat must convert a clean trial into share against an entrenched incumbent, and its own safety table fights the conversion story.

  1. The 80% dose-reduction rate contradicts the differentiation pitch.RINGSIDE's delivered-dose reality (80% reductions, 20% AE discontinuations, 57% grade 3/4) is the number payers, physicians and FDA reviewers will set against the "better-tolerated GSI" narrative. Cross-trial, DeFi's reductions were 42%. FACT (both datasets as reported)
  2. Ovarian toxicity is a class effect with incomplete reversibility data.56% incidence in premenopausal women; 55% resolved at cut. Long-term fertility outcomes are unknown, the population skews young and female, and FDA labeled the same risk prominently for Ogsiveo. Label language here is a commercial variable, not a footnote. FACT / GAP (long-term follow-up)

  4. AdCom optionality inside the review.No advisory committee announced as of Oct 20, 2026 — but FDA can convene one late in a cycle, and a class with this safety signature is a plausible candidate. A convening notice is the largest single unpriced binary before PDUFA. GAP
