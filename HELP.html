02 / SCIENCE

Deuterated psilocin: the PK story and the honest failure list

Plain-language MoA, what deuteration is supposed to add, and — honestly — why it could fail. Academic literature consulted beyond company materials.

What HLP003 is, in plain language

HLP003 is a deuterated analog of psilocin. Psilocin is the active metabolite of psilocybin (the "magic mushroom" compound): when someone takes psilocybin, the body rapidly converts it to psilocin, which then activates serotonin receptors in the brain — most importantly the 5-HT2A receptor. Helus describes HLP003 as a serotonergic agonist with activity at multiple 5-HT receptors including 5-HT2A. FACT

"Deuterated" means some hydrogen atoms in the molecule have been swapped for deuterium (heavy hydrogen — same chemistry, twice the mass). The carbon–deuterium bond is harder for metabolic enzymes to break than a carbon–hydrogen bond (the kinetic isotope effect), which can slow metabolism, smooth out blood-level peaks and troughs, and reduce patient-to-patient variability. This is the same trick behind the FDA-approved deuterated drug deutetrabenazine (Austedo) for Huntington's chorea — deuteration as a medicinal-chemistry tool is precedented, though each molecule stands or falls on its own data. INTERPRETATION

The company's stated rationale: first-generation psychedelics (psilocybin, DMT) have limitations — variable pharmacokinetics, long clinic sessions, scalability — and a deuterated, purpose-built molecule can deliver the therapeutic pharmacology with a cleaner, more practical profile. HLP003 is oral; its sibling HLP004 is a deuterated DMT (DMT-d10) given intramuscularly, with acute effects lasting ~90 minutes and discharge readiness within ~3 hours in Phase 1. FACT

Why the field thinks this could work for depression

  • Randomized evidence exists for the drug class. Psilocybin has beaten control arms in randomized trials for MDD and treatment-resistant depression: Davis et al. (JAMA Psychiatry 2021) in MDD; Goodwin et al. (NEJM 2022) — the 233-patient COMPASS trial of 25 mg vs 10 mg vs 1 mg psilocybin in TRD; Carhart-Harris et al. (NEJM 2021) — psilocybin vs escitalopram; Raison et al. (JAMA 2023) — single-dose psilocybin in MDD; von Rotz et al. (eClinicalMedicine 2023). FACT HLP003 is a chemical cousin, not the same molecule — class evidence is supportive, not dispositive. INTERPRETATION
  • Neuroplasticity hypothesis. Psychedelics promote structural and functional neural plasticity in preclinical models — Ly et al. (Cell Reports 2018) coined the "psychoplastogen" framing. The theory: a 5-HT2A-driven plasticity window lets entrenched depressive circuits reorganize, producing durable benefit after intermittent dosing rather than daily drug exposure. Company materials invoke neuroplasticity as the rationale. FACT Whether human antidepressant durability actually flows from plasticity (vs. expectancy, or the psychological experience itself) is unresolved in the literature. GAP
  • Adjunctive positioning is pharmacologically coherent. A completed drug–drug interaction study showed HLP003 did not meaningfully alter plasma levels of six CYP450 substrates, suggesting low PK interaction risk with SSRIs/SNRIs — the background meds in PARADIGM. FACT
  • IP moat. 350+ patent applications filed, 100+ granted worldwide covering the NSA platform. FACT

Why it could fail — the honest list

  • The target is not novel. HLP003 hits the same 5-HT2A pharmacology as psilocybin. If COMPASS's psilocybin succeeds in TRD, HLP003 must differentiate on profile/convenience, not mechanism; if the class stumbles at FDA, HLP003 inherits the overhang. The deuteration is a PK optimization, not a new biology. INTERPRETATION
  • Functional unblinding. This is the field's central methodological wound — and the FDA's. Psychedelic effects are unmistakable, so patients (and raters) can usually tell active from placebo; expectancy then inflates the measured drug–placebo separation. The FDA flagged exactly this in rejecting Lykos's MDMA application (adcomm 9–2 on efficacy, 10–1 on benefit–risk; ~90% of MDMA-arm subjects guessed their assignment correctly). Muthukumaraswamy et al. (Expert Rev Clin Pharmacol 2021) formalized the blinding/expectancy critique for psychedelic RCTs. PARADIGM uses an inactive placebo in both pivotal trials — the design most vulnerable to this critique. FACT INTERPRETATION — no active-placebo or low-dose control arm was included.
  • Phase 2 was small and its long-term data uncontrolled. The primary Phase 2 readout was n=34 (24 active pooled across 12/16 mg, 10 placebo) with a 13.75-point MADRS separation at 3 weeks (p<0.0001); the widely quoted 14.08-point separation (p=0.0005, Cohen's d=2.15) was the earlier interim single-dose 12 mg analysis, not the pooled readout. The celebrated 12-month figures — ~23-point MADRS reduction, 100% response, 71% remission — come from 7 patients who received two 16 mg doses and stayed in open-label follow-up, with the placebo arm crossed over to active. Large effects in tiny, selected samples routinely shrink in Phase 3; psychiatry is littered with such examples (Sage's zuranolone in MDD is the recent cautionary tale). FACT INTERPRETATION
  • Adjunctive serotonergic load. HLP003 is dosed on top of SSRIs/SNRIs. No PK interaction was found, but pharmacodynamic serotonergic risk (serotonin syndrome spectrum) is inherent to stacking serotonergic agents — the trials will have to show the safety database is clean, and regulators will scrutinize it. INTERPRETATION
  • No published evidence that deuteration improves the therapeutic index here. The deuteration story is PK elegance; whether it translates into better efficacy, safety, or durability versus plain psilocin/psilocybin in humans has not been shown in any peer-reviewed publication identified. GAP
  • Durability claim rests on uncontrolled follow-up. The 12-month Phase 2 data have no concurrent control; natural course, regression to the mean, and concomitant antidepressants all confound. EXTEND (up to 1 year, blinded, with redosing provisions) is designed to generate controlled durability data — it does not exist yet. FACT INTERPRETATION

04 / TRIAL DESIGN

PARADIGM: the trial design behind the Q4 binary

The two pivotal trials, the extension, and what the statistics do and don't tell us.

Program architecture

PARADIGM (launched November 13, 2024) = two 12-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pivotal trials plus EXTEND, a blinded long-term extension (up to ~1 year, with redosing provisions for non-responders/relapsers). Combined enrollment target ≈ 550. FACT

APPROACH — the first pivotal (the Q4 2026 binary)

ElementDetail
DesignRandomized 1:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled; n=223 enrolled (enrollment completed ahead of schedule, announced July 21, 2026). FACT
ArmsHLP003 16 mg vs inactive placebo; two doses, Day 1 and Day 22. FACT
PopulationAdults 18–85, first MDD episode before age 60; moderate-to-severe MDD (MADRS ≥ 24, CGI-S ≥ 4); inadequate response (<50% improvement) on a stable antidepressant dose. Excludes treatment-resistant MDD (≥2 failed antidepressants), psychotic/bipolar disorders, significant suicide risk (C-SSRS), psychedelic exposure within 12 months (or ≥10 lifetime uses), and ketamine/esketamine within 6 months. FACT
Primary endpointChange from baseline in MADRS total score at Day 42 (6 weeks after first dose) vs placebo. FACT
Key secondarySame MADRS measure at Day 84 (12 weeks). FACT
Baseline (n=202 reported)Mean MADRS 33.1 (HLP003) / 33.3 (placebo); median age 44.0 / 50.0; 64.4% female each arm. (Psych Congress 2026 posters.) FACT
PoweringNot disclosed. CMO Amir Inamdar has said the company has not disclosed formal powering assumptions. GAP At ~111 patients/arm, the trial has roughly 80% power to detect a standardized effect of d≈0.38 (two-sample t-test, α=0.05 two-sided) — i.e., it is powered for a COMPASS-like ~3–4 point MADRS separation (assuming a typical MADRS SD of ~8–10), not only for large effects. (A 2026-10-10 logic audit corrected an earlier draft that wrongly described the trial as powered only for Phase-2-magnitude effects — d≈2 would need ≈6 patients/arm.) A second, independent attenuation route: Phase 2's signal was measured at 3 weeks, while APPROACH's primary endpoint is at 6 weeks (Day 42) — week-3-to-week-6 fade is another way the Phase 2 prior can mislead. INTERPRETATION

What Phase 2 actually showed (the prior)

ReadoutResult
Topline (Nov 30, 2023; n=34)12 mg + 16 mg pooled: mean MADRS improvement 13.75 pts at 3 weeks vs placebo, p<0.0001; interim single-dose analysis showed 14.08-pt separation (p=0.0005, Cohen's d=2.15) with 53.3% response on active vs 0% on placebo. Two 12 mg doses: 79% response and 79% remission at 6 weeks (n=22 active). No treatment-related SAEs; AEs mild–moderate, self-limiting. FACT
4-month update75% response/remission rates sustained at 4 months. (Company PR.) FACT
12-month update (Nov 18, 2024)Two 16 mg doses (n=7 active-active): mean MADRS change ≈ −23 pts; 100% responders, 71% remitters (the two non-remitters scored MADRS 11, just above the ≤10 remission cutoff). Two 12 mg doses (n=10): −18 pts; 60% response, 50% remission. No new AEs, no suicidality signals. Open-label, uncontrolled, high attrition — treat as hypothesis-generating, not as a Phase 3 prior. FACT INTERPRETATION
Statistical reality check. A 14-point MADRS separation with d>2 in n=34 is an extraordinary signal — and extraordinary small-sample signals are precisely the ones that attenuate in larger trials (winner's curse). The registrational bar that matters is the one COMPASS just cleared in TRD: a 3.6-point separation at p<0.001 (n=258). APPROACH at n=223 needs a separation in the low-to-mid single digits, cleanly significant, to be viable — a far cry from Phase 2's 14 points, and well within the range where unblinding-driven expectancy could be doing real work. INTERPRETATION
05 / CLINICAL & REGULATORY

Breakthrough designation in a cautious FDA

Where the program stands with regulators, and what the FDA's psychedelic posture means for it.

  • FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation for HLP003 in adjunctive MDD, granted March 13, 2024 — provides Fast Track benefits (rolling NDA submission) and intensive FDA guidance on trial design; priority review is a separate FDA decision made at filing. BTD is not an approval and does not lower the efficacy bar. FACT
  • Trial design was aligned with FDA before PARADIGM launched (company statement, Nov 2024). FACT Details of that alignment (e.g., whether FDA accepted the inactive-placebo design without reservation) are not public. GAP
  • The Lykos precedent (Aug 2024). FDA issued a complete response letter for MDMA-assisted therapy in PTSD after an advisory committee voted 9–2 against efficacy and 10–1 that benefits did not outweigh risks. Central issues: functional unblinding, expectancy bias, and whether psychotherapy contributed to benefit. FDA requested another Phase 3. FACT The decision was application-specific, but it put every subsequent psychedelic sponsor on notice about blinding and trial conduct. INTERPRETATION
  • FDA psychedelic guidance. Draft guidance issued June 2023; finalized July 2026 (per Yale Journal on Regulation). It explicitly warns that perceptual effects can defeat traditional placebos and suggests alternatives (e.g., sub-perceptual/psychoactive comparators, dose–response designs). EMBRACE's 16 mg / 8 mg / placebo structure partially answers the dose–response suggestion; APPROACH does not. FACT INTERPRETATION
  • Regulatory tailwinds in 2026. FDA held a public hearing on psychedelic therapy access in September 2026. Following an April 2026 executive order on psychedelic treatments, Commissioner Makary issued Commissioner's National Priority Vouchers (1–2-month review target) on Apr 24, 2026 to three Breakthrough-designated psychedelic programs — COMPASS's COMP360, Usona's psilocybin, and Transcend's methylone. COMPASS is in a rolling NDA for COMP360 with final submission targeted Q4 2026. FACT A first-ever psychedelic approval would de-risk the regulatory path conceptually — but also set the comparator bar. INTERPRETATION
  • Controlled-substance reality. HLP003/HLP004 are Schedule I (US), Schedule III (Canada CDSA), Class A/Schedule 1 (UK). Approval would require DEA rescheduling proceedings and a REMS-like supervised-administration framework (the Spravato precedent). This is a post-binary commercial consideration, kept lean per the mandate. FACT
  • Company-stated path: if APPROACH data hold, Helus aims to file an NDA in 2028 (reported via company commentary, trade press). UNVERIFIED — no formal NDA-filing guidance appears in the company's primary filings reviewed.

10 / RISKS

What could sink the binary

Ordered by potential impact on the binary, not by likelihood.

  1. Phase 2 → Phase 3 translation risk is extreme here. n=34 primary readout; the 12-month durability story is n=7, uncontrolled, with the placebo arm crossed over. Winner's-curse attenuation is the base-rate expectation in psychiatry Phase 3. INTERPRETATION
  2. Inactive placebo in both pivotal trials. The design maximally exposed to functional unblinding — the exact issue at the heart of the Lykos CRL. A positive readout will face the "was it the drug or the trip + expectancy?" question from FDA reviewers and skeptics alike. FACT INTERPRETATION
  3. Powering undisclosed; n=223 is modest but not underpowered on paper. ~111/arm gives roughly 80% power for d≈0.38 — so a COMPASS-like ~3–4 point MADRS separation (assuming SD ~8–10) is within the trial's detection range, not borderline. The real risk is the undisclosed assumption: if the company powered for a larger effect and the true effect is smaller, power falls off fast — and it won't say what it assumed. GAP INTERPRETATION
  4. CEO churn: three transitions in ~18 months. Drysdale (2020–2025) → Cola (ousted at the board's request, Apr 2026) → So (interim, twice) → Halstead (Aug 2026). Halstead's Intra-Cellular/J&J pedigree is strong, but the turnover pattern warrants attention, not celebration. FACT INTERPRETATION
  5. Financing wall before the second binary. ~4.5 quarters of cash at current burn; the $100M ATM is live and unused (as of Jun 30). A raise is highly likely before/during the EMBRACE window — timing and pricing depend entirely on APPROACH. INTERPRETATION
  6. Baseline age imbalance. Median age 44.0 (HLP003) vs 50.0 (placebo) among the 202 with reported baselines. Probably noise, but a 6-year median gap in a 223-patient depression trial is worth noting when the readout lands. FACT INTERPRETATION
  7. TRD excluded — narrower label, selected population. Excluding ≥2-failure patients (and recent psychedelic/ketamine users) enriches for response but limits generalizability and the eventual label's breadth. FACT INTERPRETATION
  8. Heavy RSU-based compensation just vested. July 2026: 4.1M RSUs granted (4.0M immediately vested, partially locked up); August: 1.175M RSUs + 425k PSUs to two new officers. Alignment or dilution depends on your priors — the share count did the talking (+11.1M shares in six weeks). FACT INTERPRETATION
  9. No psychedelic has ever been approved. The entire class is pre-precedent at FDA (COMPASS pending). BTD ≠ approval; the finalized July 2026 psychedelic guidance raises the methodological bar. FACT
  10. Deuteration benefit is theoretical in humans. No peer-reviewed data show deuterated psilocin beats psilocybin/psilocin on efficacy, safety, or durability. The "better molecule" thesis is PK reasoning, not clinical fact. GAP

Scenario analyses

  • Mixed readout (one trial hits, one misses): the most analytically interesting branch. A hit on APPROACH + miss on EMBRACE (or vice versa) forces the question the field dreads: was the positive the fluke, or the negative the underpowered one? FDA has approved on one strong pivotal plus supportive data in psychiatry before — but with the unblinding cloud, a split decision likely means a third trial or a narrowed path, plus a financing from ambiguity. INTERPRETATION
  • Positive but "soft" APPROACH (p<0.05, small separation, e.g., 2–3 points): statistically a win, commercially/regulatorily debatable. Expect fierce debate over clinical meaningfulness (the COMPASS 3.6-point precedent cuts both ways) and an immediate focus on EMBRACE's dose-response leg to arbitrate. INTERPRETATION
  • Safety signal: not the base case (Phase 1/2 database is clean: no treatment-related SAEs, no suicidality signal), but serotonergic stacking on SSRIs/SNRIs and the class's suicidality scrutiny mean any imbalance in suicidality-related events would dominate the readout narrative regardless of efficacy. INTERPRETATION
  • Financing-first scenario: company taps the $100M ATM before APPROACH data (unlikely given the readout is weeks away) or immediately after — from strength on a hit, from weakness on a miss. Warrant exercises at $10.11 could bring ~$121M of cash if the price holds above strike — cash-generating but dilutive, at the cost of ~16% share expansion. INTERPRETATION
