02 / SCIENCE

DHODH blockade plus Nurr1—and the lesion-to-relapse gap

Plain-language mechanism

Vidofludimus calcium has two claimed pharmacologic actions:

  1. Selective DHODH inhibition (anti-inflammatory / anti-viral). Dihydroorotate dehydrogenase is a mitochondrial enzyme in the rate-limiting step of de novo pyrimidine synthesis. Rapidly dividing cells — activated T and B lymphocytes, and virus-infected cells — depend on this pathway; blocking it preferentially starves activated lymphocytes while resting immune cells, which can use salvage pathways, are largely spared. The net effect is immunomodulation without broad immunosuppression. FACTMechanism as described in the EMPhASIS publication (Fox et al., Ann Clin Transl Neurol 2022) and the FDA label of the class precedent teriflunomide (Aubagio).
  2. Nurr1 activation (postulated neuroprotection). Nuclear receptor-related 1 (Nurr1, NR4A2) is a transcription factor involved in neuronal survival and anti-neuroinflammatory signaling. Immunic, with Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich, reported in a peer-reviewed paper that vidofludimus calcium is a potent Nurr1 agonist at low concentrations, selective over the related receptors Nur77 and NOR-1, and that other DHODH inhibitors tested did not activate Nurr1. FACTSource: Vietor et al., J Med Chem 2023;66(9):6391–6402.

Preclinical support for the neuroprotective leg: vidofludimus calcium enhanced Nurr1 target-gene expression (BDNF, SOD1, TH, VMAT2) in neuronal/microglial cell lines, protected neurons under pro-apoptotic conditions, and attenuated disease severity with reduced plasma neurofilament light chain in experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE) mouse models. A 2026 poster reports the pro-survival effect in human neuronal cells is abolished by Nurr1 knockout. FACTSource: company ECTRIMS 2024 ePoster P1410; MSToronto2026 poster P1273 (company PR, Oct 7, 2026). Preclinical — not human efficacy.

Why it could fail — the scientific bear case

INTERPRETATION1. The DHODH efficacy ceiling is modest. The class precedent, teriflunomide (Aubagio, approved 2012 for relapsing MS), is an oral DHODH inhibitor with placebo-controlled efficacy in the moderate range — its pivotal TEMSO trial showed roughly a one-third relative reduction in annualized relapse rate (O'Connor et al., N Engl J Med 2011). Vidofludimus is more selective (teriflunomide's diarrhea, alopecia, and liver signals are attributed to off-target kinase interactions, per Cleveland Clinic's review of the EMPhASIS data), but selectivity improves tolerability, not necessarily potency — the anti-inflammatory effect still runs through the same enzyme. If 30 mg vidofludimus behaves like a cleaner teriflunomide, the relapse effect size may be real but modest, and the ENSURE primary (time to first relapse vs placebo) must clear statistical significance in both trials, not just one. Class efficacy: TEMSO (O'Connor et al., NEJM 2011); teriflunomide side-effect attribution: Cleveland Clinic ConsultQD review of EMPhASIS. Effect-size judgment: interpretation.
INTERPRETATION2. MRI lesions ≠ relapses. EMPhASIS proved lesion suppression (70% fewer combined unique active lesions at 30 mg, p<.0001). But the Phase 3 primary is time to first relapse — a clinical endpoint with more noise (relapse ascertainment, pseudo-relapses, regional adjudication variability across 100+ sites in 15 countries). Lesion effects do not always translate one-for-one into relapse effects, and the trial is powered on relapse events, not patients. EMPhASIS: Fox et al., ACTN 2022. ENSURE design: company deck Feb 2025. Translation risk: interpretation.
INTERPRETATION3. Nurr1 neuroprotection is unproven in humans. The neuroprotective leg rests on cell lines, EAE mice, and biomarker movement (serum NfL reductions in CALLIPER). CALLIPER's actual primary endpoint — change in brain volume — was missed (trade-press reporting of full data). A drug can lower NfL without changing clinical disability trajectories; regulators do not accept NfL as a surrogate for approval in MS. CALLIPER primary miss: multiplesclerosisnewstoday.com (trade press) — UNVERIFIED pending peer-reviewed publication. NfL regulatory status: interpretation of FDA precedent.
FACT4. DHODH is a teratogenicity-flagged pathway. Teriflunomide carries a boxed warning for hepatotoxicity and is contraindicated in pregnancy (embryofetal toxicity in animals); leflunomide (the RA prodrug of teriflunomide) carries similar warnings. Whether vidofludimus calcium's selectivity spares it from class labeling is a regulatory question that will only be answered at NDA review — and the label negotiation, not the readout, will determine the commercial warning box. Aubagio (teriflunomide) US prescribing information; Arava (leflunomide) US prescribing information.
GAP5. Dose–response is thin. EMPhASIS tested 10, 30, and 45 mg; only 30 and 45 mg worked on MRI, and 45 mg added liver-enzyme elevations (2/69) without better efficacy — hence 30 mg for Phase 3. There is no published evidence that 30 mg is at the top of the dose–response curve for relapse prevention rather than just the best-tolerated active dose. Dose rationale: Practical Neurology summary of EMPhASIS; company CMO statement.

Statistical plan & powering — what matters

  • Event-driven. The interim was triggered at approximately half of the planned first-relapse events — the trial reads out on relapse count, not calendar time. FACT
  • Futility only. The IDMC answered two questions: (1) are futility criteria met? No. (2) should sample size increase? No — continue as planned. Conditional power at interim supported continuation. FACT
  • INTERPRETATION What "continue" does and doesn't mean. Passing futility rules out a disaster (conditional power below the futility boundary); it does not confirm the trial will hit significance, and the sponsor never saw unblinded data. The no-upsize call means observed event rates tracked close enough to plan — mildly reassuring on operations, neutral on efficacy.
  • GAP Target number of relapse events, assumed hazard ratio, and alpha-spending details were not disclosed in any source reviewed. Without the assumed treatment effect, outside powering math is guesswork.
  • INTERPRETATION Placebo-controlled in 2026 RMS is a design risk. With >20 approved DMTs, placebo relapse rates in modern RMS trials run lower than historical norms, and relapses are adjudicated across 100+ sites in 15 countries. Low event rates + adjudication noise = the classic way a real-but-modest drug misses.

Readouts to date

EMPhASIS — Phase 2 RRMS (NCT03846219)

  • 268 patients randomized (36 centers, 4 European countries); 24-week double-blind; cohorts: 30 mg, 45 mg, 10 mg, placebo QD. FACT
  • Primary + key secondaries met: 30 mg → −70% cumulative CUA lesions vs placebo (p<.0001); 45 mg → −62% (p=.0002); 10 mg → −13% (pooled cohorts). Gd+ lesion reductions similar. FACT
  • Serum NfL reduced; signal on preventing confirmed disability worsening. FACT
  • Safety ≈ placebo: no SAEs at 30 mg; 45 mg had 3 treatment-related events (2 liver-enzyme elevations, 1 rash); discontinuations 2.8% (30 mg) / 5.8% (45 mg) / 7.2% (placebo); no increased infection rate, no liver/blood-cell signals at 30 mg. FACT
  • Open-label extension (n=182, 144 weeks / ~3 yrs): 92.3% free of 12-week CDW; 92.7% free of 24-week CDW. Of 29 CDW events, 44.8% relapse-associated, only 13.8% progression-independent-of-relapse (PIRA). FACT

Source: Fox et al., Ann Clin Transl Neurol 2022 (peer-reviewed); OLE data via company/ECTRIMS 2025 presentations (trade-press reporting).

CALLIPER — Phase 2 progressive MS (NCT05054140)

  • 467 patients with progressive MS (PPMS + active/non-active SPMS). FACT
  • Interim (n=203, 24 weeks): serum NfL −22.4% vs placebo (p=.01, post hoc), consistent across PMS subtypes, age and EDSS strata. FACTPost-hoc analysis; ECTRIMS 2024.
  • Full data: primary endpoint (change in brain volume) not met; risk of 24-week confirmed disability worsening reduced by 23.8%, including in non-active patients. UNVERIFIEDPrimary-endpoint miss via multiplesclerosisnewstoday.com; no peer-reviewed publication located. The 23.8% 24-week CDW reduction is in Immunic's own 8-K Ex-99.1 (Aug 7, 2025; data first announced Apr 2025).
  • Oct 7, 2026: company announced new CALLIPER open-label extension data — disability outcomes favoring continuous vidofludimus calcium treatment maintained through extension week 48, with low treatment discontinuation — to be presented at MSToronto2026 (Oct 21–23, 2026, Toronto). FACTCompany PR, Oct 7, 2026 (PRNewswire). Full data pending conference presentation; not yet peer-reviewed.
  • INTERPRETATION Mixed: biomarker and disability-worsening signals with a missed primary is the profile of a drug with possible neuroprotective activity — supportive of the PPMS Phase 3 option, not proof of it.

INTERPRETATIONWhere vidofludimus would slot — if it works. Its clinical pitch is oral convenience + placebo-like tolerability + anti-inflammatory efficacy in the teriflunomide-or-better range + a neuroprotective story no oral currently owns. It does not need to beat anti-CD20s on efficacy to be viable; it needs to be a tolerable oral that delays relapses and can credibly claim disability biology. The commercial threat is a BTK inhibitor reaching market first with a progressive-MS label (fenebrutinib) — but that is a post-binary, commercial-phase concern, deliberately out of scope here.

Ranked red flags

1
Translation risk: MRI → relapses. The entire investment case rests on Phase 2 lesion suppression converting into Phase 3 relapse prevention against placebo in 2,221 patients across 15 countries. Lesion effects and relapse effects can diverge; the primary is adjudicated clinical events, not imaging. INTERPRETATION
2
Dilution overhang is extreme. 42.5M economic shares (common + pre-funded) vs 13.6M basic, up to ~76M if everything is exercised; 22.9M warrants at $8.73 exercisable within 30 days of data (~$200M proceeds — see §8); a 5% net-sales royalty already sold. Per-share economics on any outcome are far thinner than the headline price suggests, and a win brings mechanical supply. FACTCounts and strikes: Q2 2026 10-Q.
3
Single-asset dependence + prior indication failure. CALDOSE-1 (UC, 2022) missed its primary and the program was shelved without a partner — proof this molecule's effects don't translate automatically. IMU-856/IMU-381 are pre-revenue early stage. FACT
4
Neuroprotection narrative is preclinical + biomarker. CALLIPER missed its brain-volume primary; the disability-worsening signal (−23.8% 24-week CDW) is company-reported (8-K Ex-99.1, Aug 2025), post-hoc-flavored, and unpublished peer-reviewed. No human proof that Nurr1 activation changes disability. UNVERIFIED / INTERPRETATION
5
Management turnover into the binary. Founder-CEO Daniel Vitt stepped down May 2026 (now consulting + Chair of SAB); new CEO Erik Lundgren is a commercial operator (ex-Genentech, Ocrevus launch) with no history with this asset; CMO changed to Michael Panzara. Leadership that ran the trials is partially out the door before data. FACT
6
Placebo-controlled RMS trials in 2026. Modern placebo relapse rates are low and adjudication across 100+ sites / 15 countries adds noise — the classic setup for a real-but-modest drug to miss statistical significance. INTERPRETATION
7
Runway covers the readout, not the program. Cash to late 2027 funds ENSURE completion and PPMS Phase 3 start; a 3.5–4-year PPMS program plus commercial build needs far more capital → future dilution is structural, not situational. INTERPRETATION
8
1-for-10 reverse split (Apr 2026) — signals how far the stock had fallen (52-wk low $5.06 pre-data) and resets the technical base, but splits don't change value. FACT
9
Class labeling overhang. DHODH inhibitors carry liver/pregnancy warnings (teriflunomide boxed warning); whether vidofludimus's selectivity earns a cleaner label is decided at NDA review, not at readout. FACT / INTERPRETATION
10
BTK inhibitors could own the "progressive" narrative first. Fenebrutinib's priority-review NDA (RMS + PPMS, accepted Sep 2026) could deliver an oral progressive-MS label before vidofludimus's PPMS program reads out (~2030). Post-binary concern; not a readout risk. INTERPRETATION
